# PFM-23: Monolith Instability due to Energy Dissipator Failure

Flow that is not adequately dissipated in the energy dissipator can erode the material downstream of it. A headcut then progresses upstream under
the energy dissipator and the upstream slabs, and the concrete monolith becomes unstable. This sequence is potential failure mode 23 (PFM-23),
Monolith Instability due to Energy Dissipator Failure, and the PFM-23 page estimates its system response probability (SRP) at one location with an
event tree. The loading is the reservoir pool, evaluated at stages from the spillway crest through the top of gates to the top of dam.

The user opens a PFM-23 location from **Response > Failure Mode Evaluation** (see [Opening a Location](./341-locations.mdx#opening-a-location)).
[Event Trees](./344-event-trees.mdx) describes the parts that every event-tree page shares, and
[Failure Mode Pages](./342-failure-mode-pages.mdx) the parts that every failure mode page shares. To evaluate a location:

When no section is selected, the section has no gate, or the gate has no closed top elevation, DST places the Top of Gates stage a third of the way from the spillway
crest to the top of dam.

Each PFM-23 location has its own cross section, stages, node probabilities and results, and PFM-23 has no limit on the number of locations. A copied
location keeps the cross section, stages, node probabilities and rationales of the original (see [Copying a Location](./341-locations.mdx#copying-a-location)).

## User-Defined SRP

With **User defined** selected in the toolbar, the user enters the SRP curve as **Pool Elevation (ft)** and **Probability** pairs in place of the
event tree. DST keeps the stages, node probabilities and rationales, and shows them again when the user selects **Event tree** (see
[User-Defined SRP](./343-system-response.mdx#user-defined-srp)).

## Common Problems

cannot be edited or a refused cross section, are described in [Common Problems](./344-event-trees.mdx#common-problems) on the Event Trees
page. Problems every failure mode page shares, such as no usable cross sections or a deleted cross section, are described in
[Common Problems](./342-failure-mode-pages.mdx#common-problems) on the Failure Mode Pages page. This page adds its own.
