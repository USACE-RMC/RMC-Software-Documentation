# Major Findings

The Major Findings module holds the screening team's written conclusions: the characterization of the risk, the uncertainties that matter most, a
summary of the findings and the team's recommendations. Each is written as formatted text on its own page. Reviewers read these pages with the rest
of the screening during review (see [Chapter 34, Review](./34-review.mdx)).

The module has four pages, reached from **Major Findings** in the module navigation: Risk Characterization, Major Uncertainties, Summary of Findings
and Recommendations. The first three are required before the screening can be submitted; Recommendations is optional. All four pages share one
layout, described below, and differ only in their purpose and prompts.

## The Major Findings Pages

Each Major Findings page has a purpose statement, a **What to consider** button and a text editor.

### The text editor

The editor accepts paragraphs, line breaks within a paragraph, bold and italic text, and bulleted and numbered lists, which can be nested with
**Indent** and **Outdent**. Text pasted from another program keeps only these formats. The editor saves the text after the user pauses typing and again when the user leaves the editor (see
[Saving](./04-working-in-dst.mdx#saving)). A page's text that grows too long to store is not saved, and DST reports the problem with the page's name first, for example
"Risk characterization: The text is too long to save."

Changing a page's text clears that page's verification only; the other Major Findings pages keep theirs (see
[Human Verification](./04-working-in-dst.mdx#human-verification)). While the screening's inputs are locked, the editor is read-only (see
[Input Lock](./04-working-in-dst.mdx#input-lock)).

## Risk Characterization

The Risk Characterization page, at **Major Findings > Risk Characterization**, holds the team's case for the risk estimate. Its purpose statement
reads "Tie the hazards, system response and consequences together to build the case for the risk estimate." The page is required.

**What to consider** lists:

- Which failure modes and loading ranges drive the risk, and why
- How the hazard, system response and consequences combine in the estimate
- The evidence behind the key estimates

## Major Uncertainties

The Major Uncertainties page, at **Major Findings > Major Uncertainties**, holds the uncertainties that matter most to the result. Its purpose
statement reads "Describe the uncertainties that matter most and how they could change the risk estimate or a dam safety decision." The page is
required.

**What to consider** lists:

- Missing or uncertain information the estimates rest on
- Judgments the team made where the evidence was thin
- Whether new information could change the risk classification or a decision

## Summary of Findings

The Summary of Findings page, at **Major Findings > Summary of Findings**, holds the team's key findings. Its purpose statement reads "Summarize the
team's key findings from this screening." The page is required.

**What to consider** lists:

- What the team observed about the dam's condition and performance
- What each observation means for risk
- What reviewers should take away

## Recommendations

The Recommendations page, at **Major Findings > Recommendations**, holds the actions or path forward the team recommends. Its purpose statement reads
"Recommend actions or a path forward for this dam." The page is optional.

**What to consider** lists:

- Investigations or data that would reduce the major uncertainties
- Monitoring, further analysis or other next steps

:::note

An empty Recommendations page has nothing to verify, shows no verification badge and does not hold back submission. Once it holds text, it must be
verified like the other pages.

:::
