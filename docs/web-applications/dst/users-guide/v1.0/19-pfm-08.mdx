# PFM-08: CLE due to Desiccation Cracking at the Crest

Potential failure mode 08 (PFM-08) is concentrated leak erosion (CLE) through a transverse crack that forms when the soil at the embankment crest
dries and shrinks, and extends down from the crest. The loading is the reservoir (headwater) whenever the pool rises above the bottom of the crack.
The PFM-08 page characterizes the climate and the crest material, reads the crack and the seepage pathway from an embankment cross section, and
produces the system response probability (SRP) curve for each location. [Chapter 11, Failure Mode Evaluation](./11-failure-mode-evaluation.mdx#the-failure-mode-page) describes the parts of the page that every failure mode page shares.

The user opens a PFM-08 location from the Failure Mode Evaluation page (**Response > Failure Mode Evaluation**, then the location under PFM-08; see
[Opening a location](./11-failure-mode-evaluation.mdx#opening-a-location)). The page heading reads **08 - CLE due to Desiccation Cracking at the
Crest**. <FigReference figKey="dst-users-guide-fig-pfm-08-page" /> shows the page, and
<TableReference tableKey="dst-users-guide-table-pfm-08-regions" /> describes each numbered region.

## Desiccation Cracking and Failure Pathway

The **Desiccation cracking and failure pathway** section sets the drying conditions and the crest material, and shows the crack and the pathway DST
reads from the cross section. The section follows the characterization pattern (see [Characterization](./11-failure-mode-evaluation.mdx#characterization)). On a wide screen the inputs, readings and pathway figure sit side by side; on
a narrower screen the readings move to a band below them. This page has no overrides tray.

### Pathway figure

The pathway figure draws the selected cross section to scale with its material zones, a legend of the materials present, and a shaded band for the
headwater analysis range. When a crack forms, the figure draws it down from the crest and draws the failure pathway across the section at the bottom
of the crack. When the section has a drain or filter below the crest, the pathway is split at the top of the filter into a "Below-filter failure
pathway" and an "Above-filter failure pathway", or labeled "Failure pathway, all pools" when the filter caps the pathway at every pool; otherwise a
single "Failure pathway" is drawn. Callouts at the side give the elevation of the **Crest**, the **Top of filter**, the **Bottom of crack** and the
**Top of wall** of a cutoff wall below the crest.

Until results exist, the caption reads "The pathway is read from the drawing once the model has run." followed by the calculation state; when no
crack forms it reads "No desiccation crack forms at this crest."; otherwise it reads "Drawn to scale from the selected cross section." with the
elevation units. Without a selected cross section the figure shows a placeholder. [Characterization](./11-failure-mode-evaluation.mdx#characterization) describes the cross-section figure.

### Node assumption cards

A row of cards under the pathway figure states what DST assumes at each node of the PFM-08 event tree, from the inputs and the current results:

- **Flaw** appears only when continuous cracking is observed, and reads "Continuous transverse cracking has been observed. The presence of a
  continuous flaw was assigned a probability of 1."
- **Initiation** reads "Initiation is assumed to occur for all pools that exceed the bottom of the crack elevation. A probability of zero is assigned
  for all pools below the bottom of the crack." or "No transverse crack forms, so erosion cannot initiate at any pool." when no crack forms.
- **Filter compatibility** summarizes the materials the crack crosses below the top of the filter and the filter they are evaluated against, or
  reports that the section has no filter.
- **Sidewall support** describes whether the crack walls stay open, evaluated once for the full pathway, or separately below and above the top of
  the filter.
- **Flow limitation** appears only when an upstream element, such as a cutoff wall, limits flow, and describes it.
- **Crack filling** appears only when shell material may fill the crack.

## System Response

The **System Response** section shows the PFM-08 SRP curve, its table and the eight nodal tables, as described in Chapter 11, Failure Mode Evaluation ([System Response](./11-failure-mode-evaluation.mdx#system-response), [SRP charts](./11-failure-mode-evaluation.mdx#srp-charts), [Nodal tables](./11-failure-mode-evaluation.mdx#nodal-tables)). The chart plots **System Response Probability** on a logarithmic axis from 1E-6
against **Headwater Elevation (ft)**, and **Download SRP curves as a CSV file** exports the curves.
<TableReference tableKey="dst-users-guide-table-pfm-08-nodes" /> lists the nodes. A pool-independent node takes one value for every headwater.
[Nodal revisions](./11-failure-mode-evaluation.mdx#nodal-revisions) describes revising a node, and [Calculation currency](./11-failure-mode-evaluation.mdx#calculation-currency) describes the notice about out-of-date results.

## User-Defined SRP

With **User Defined SRP** selected, the page replaces the characterization and system response sections with a grid of headwater elevations and
probabilities and its chart, with **Headwater Elevation (ft)** on the x axis. [User-defined SRP](./11-failure-mode-evaluation.mdx#user-defined-srp) and [Active SRP](./11-failure-mode-evaluation.mdx#active-srp) describe the grid and the switch between modes.

## Failure Mode Summary

[Chapter 11, Failure Mode Evaluation](./11-failure-mode-evaluation.mdx#failure-mode-summary) describes the **Failure mode summary** tray at the bottom of the page. When Failure Mode Screening question 9 is answered **Unknown**, its
**Unknowns** section lists "Transverse cracking observed" with "Treated as not observed." Its **Parameter overrides** section lists the governing
crest material when it differs from the default.
