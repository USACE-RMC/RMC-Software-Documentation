# Hazards

The Hazards module holds the loading the screening is evaluated against. Its three pages are [Flood Hazard](./201-flood-hazard.mdx), the annual exceedance probability of
each reservoir stage; [Stage Duration](./202-stage-duration.mdx), the share of time the reservoir sits at or above each stage; and [Seismic Hazard](./203-seismic-hazard.mdx), the annual exceedance
probability of each level of ground motion. The failure mode pages, Consequences and Risk Analysis read their loading from these pages: the flood-loaded
failure modes from Flood Hazard, and the seismic failure modes from Seismic Hazard and Stage Duration, which also supplies the normal pool
elevation.

Each page has its own verification badge, and changing an input clears its verification (see
[Human Verification](./040-working-in-dst.mdx#human-verification)). When a change makes the results of a later page out of date, DST clears those
results and says so (see [Automatic Calculation](./040-working-in-dst.mdx#automatic-calculation)). Each page also has a summary tray at the bottom
for its justification and supporting documents (see [Summary Tray](./040-working-in-dst.mdx#summary-tray) and
[Supporting Documents](./150-documents.mdx#supporting-documents)).
