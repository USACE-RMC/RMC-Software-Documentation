# Major Uncertainties

On the Major Uncertainties page (**Major Findings > Major Uncertainties**), the team describes the uncertainties that matter most to the result.
The page is required. Its purpose statement reads "Describe the uncertainties that matter most and how they could change the risk estimate or a dam
safety decision."

**What to consider** lists:

- Missing or uncertain information the estimates rest on
- Judgments the team made where the evidence was thin
- Whether new information could change the risk classification or a decision

While the editor is empty, it shows "Describe the major uncertainties…", and the page messages report "Describe the major uncertainties." For
writing, saving and verifying the text, see [Writing a Major Findings Page](./600-major-findings.mdx#writing-a-major-findings-page).
