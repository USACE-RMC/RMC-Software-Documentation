# PFM-08: CLE due to Desiccation Cracking at the Crest

Use this page to evaluate potential failure mode 08 (PFM-08) at a location: concentrated leak erosion (CLE) through a transverse crack that forms
when the soil at the embankment crest dries and shrinks. The loading is the reservoir (headwater) whenever the pool rises above the bottom of the
crack. The user sets the climate and confirms the crest material; DST reads the crack and the seepage pathway from an embankment cross section and
calculates the system response probability (SRP) curve. [Failure Mode Pages](./342-failure-mode-pages.mdx) and
[System Response](./343-system-response.mdx) describe the parts that every failure mode page shares.

The user opens a PFM-08 location from **Response > Failure Mode Evaluation** (see [Opening a location](./341-locations.mdx#opening-a-location)).
The page heading reads **08 - CLE due to Desiccation Cracking at the Crest**. To evaluate a location:

## Desiccation Cracking and Failure Pathway

The **Desiccation cracking and failure pathway** section sets the drying conditions and the crest material, and shows the crack and the pathway DST
reads from the cross section (see also [Characterization](./342-failure-mode-pages.mdx#characterization)). The only override on this page is the
governing crest material.

### Pathway figure

The pathway figure draws the selected cross section to scale with a materials legend and the headwater analysis range. When a crack forms, it draws
the crack down from the crest and the failure pathway across the section at the bottom of the crack, with the elevations of the **Crest**, the
**Bottom of crack** and, when present, the **Top of filter** and the **Top of wall** of a cutoff wall. When a drain or filter lies below the crest,
the pathway is split into a "Below-filter failure pathway" and an "Above-filter failure pathway", or labeled "Failure pathway, all pools" when the
filter caps the pathway at every pool.

The caption reports the state: "The pathway is read from the drawing once the model has run." before results exist, "No desiccation crack forms at
this crest." when no crack forms, and "Drawn to scale from the selected cross section." otherwise.

### Node assumption cards

The cards state what DST assumes at each node of the PFM-08 system response, from the inputs and the current results:

- **Flaw** appears only when continuous cracking is observed: "Continuous transverse cracking has been observed. The presence of a continuous flaw
  was assigned a probability of 1."
- **Initiation** reads "Initiation is assumed to occur for all pools that exceed the bottom of the crack elevation. A probability of zero is assigned
  for all pools below the bottom of the crack." or, when no crack forms, "No transverse crack forms, so erosion cannot initiate at any pool."
- **Filter compatibility** names the materials the crack crosses below the top of the filter and the filter they are evaluated against, or reports
  that the section has no filter.
- **Sidewall support** states whether the crack walls stay open, for the full pathway or separately below and above the top of the filter.
- **Flow limitation** appears only when an upstream element, such as a cutoff wall, limits flow.
- **Crack filling** appears only when shell material may fill the crack.

The **Failure mode summary** tray at the bottom of the page (see [Failure mode summary](./342-failure-mode-pages.mdx#failure-mode-summary)) lists

## Common problems

Problems every failure mode page shares, such as a system response that does not calculate, no usable cross sections, a deleted cross
section or a refused verification, are described in [Common problems](./342-failure-mode-pages.mdx#common-problems) on the Failure Mode
Pages page. This page adds its own.

**Governing crest material reads "Available once results are calculated".** The drop-down fills after the first calculation. The user resolves the
required inputs in the page messages (climate, Failure Mode Screening questions 6 and 9, and the cross section); the list then offers the crest materials.

**A reading shows "Not answered".** The Failure Mode Screening question behind it is unanswered. The reading's information button links to the
question; the user answers it there and returns to the location.

**A material or cutoff wall message names the Dam Information page.** A cutoff or seepage barrier wall needs a wall type and a top elevation before
DST can apply its flow-limitation credit; the user completes the wall on the Dam Information page (see [Dam Information](./310-dam-information.mdx)).
For materials without a USCS classification, see
[A material has no USCS classification](./342-failure-mode-pages.mdx#a-material-has-no-uscs-classification).

**The tailwater warning appears.** No action is needed: the PFM-08 SRP does not depend on tailwater.
