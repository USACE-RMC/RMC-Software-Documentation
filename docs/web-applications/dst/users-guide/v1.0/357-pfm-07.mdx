# PFM-07: CLE due to Poor Compaction Along Foundation or Abutment

Fill that is poorly compacted along its contact with the foundation or an abutment is the setting of potential failure mode 07 (PFM-07): concentrated
leak erosion (CLE) through that fill. The reservoir loads the failure mode whenever the pool rises above the flaw, and the PFM-07 page estimates the
resulting system response probability (SRP). [Failure Mode Pages](./342-failure-mode-pages.mdx) and [System Response](./343-system-response.mdx)
describe the parts every failure mode page shares.

The user opens a PFM-07 location from **Response > Failure Mode Evaluation** (see [Opening a location](./341-locations.mdx#opening-a-location)).
The page heading reads **07 - CLE due to Poor Compaction Along Foundation or Abutment**. To evaluate a location:

## Location and Cross Section

Each PFM-07 location evaluates one contact, foundation or abutment, on one cross section. The user adds, copies, renames and deletes PFM-07
locations as described in [Locations](./341-locations.mdx). [The page toolbar](./342-failure-mode-pages.mdx#the-page-toolbar) describes the toolbar,
and <TableReference tableKey="dst-users-guide-table-pfm-07-toolbar-inputs" /> lists its inputs.

:::tip

To evaluate both contacts on the same cross section, the user sets up the foundation location, copies it with **Copy location**, and changes
**Contact** to **Abutment** on the copy. A copy keeps every input of the original.

## Contact Flaw and Seepage Pathway

The contact, and how the fill against it was compacted, determine the flaw. The **Contact flaw and seepage pathway** section records both and
presents for review the pathway DST reads from the cross section. The section follows the characterization pattern (see
[Characterization](./342-failure-mode-pages.mdx#characterization)).

them. The compaction drop-down is labeled **Foundation contact zone compaction** or **Abutment contact zone compaction** to match the contact, and
**Contact zone compaction** before a contact is chosen. Its information button lists the definition of every option.

### Pathway figure

The pathway figure draws the selected cross section to scale with the "Failure pathway" line along the flaw. The pathway ends at the first drain or
filter it meets, and the legend shows whether it meets one. Labels give the elevations of the **Crest**, the **Normal pool**, the **Base of flaw**
and, for a cutoff wall below the crest, the **Top of wall**. Until DST resolves the pathway, the caption reads "The pathway is read from the drawing
once the model has run." [Characterization](./342-failure-mode-pages.mdx#characterization) describes the cross-section figure.

### Node assumption cards

The cards state what DST assumes at each node of the PFM-07 system response, from the inputs and the current results:

- **Flaw** appears only for a well-prepared contact, and reads, for example, "For a compacted soil foundation, the probability of a continuous flaw is
  zero." or "For a regular or treated rock foundation surface, the probability of a continuous flaw is zero." ("abutment" replaces "foundation" for an
  abutment contact).
- **Initiation** states that initiation is evaluated for pools above the failure pathway, and whether the critical shear stress and the initial
  pipe diameter come from defaults or from entered distributions.
- **Filter compatibility** names the drain or filter the pathway reaches and the materials evaluated against it, or reports that the pathway reaches
  no filter.
- **Roof support** names the pathway materials that can hold up the roof of an eroding pipe.
- **Flow limitation** appears only when an upstream element, such as a cutoff wall, limits flow, and describes it.
- **Crack filling** appears only when shell material may fill the flaw.

description reads "Screening defaults come from the governing initiation material and stay locked until a site-specific distribution is chosen."

## User-Defined SRP

With **User Defined SRP** selected, the page replaces the characterization and system response sections with a grid of headwater elevations and
probabilities and its chart. <FigReference figKey="dst-users-guide-fig-pfm-07-page" /> shows this mode.
[User-defined SRP](./343-system-response.mdx#user-defined-srp) and [Active SRP](./343-system-response.mdx#active-srp) describe the grid and the switch
between modes.
