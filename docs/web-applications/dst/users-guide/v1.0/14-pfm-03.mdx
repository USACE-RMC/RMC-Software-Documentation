# PFM-03: CLE due to Differential Settlement Adjacent to a Cliff or Wall

Potential failure mode 03 (PFM-03) evaluates concentrated leak erosion (CLE) through a transverse crack at the crest, where the embankment settles
differentially against a rigid face such as a cliff, an excavation, a concrete monolith or a wall. The loading is the reservoir pool, and the page plots the system
response against headwater elevation. [Chapter 11, Failure Mode Evaluation](./11-failure-mode-evaluation.mdx#the-failure-mode-page) describes the parts of the page that every failure mode page shares.

## Location and Cross Section

DST creates one PFM-03 location for each structure contact selected in Failure Mode Screening question 5 (see
[Chapter 10, Failure Mode Screening and Selection](./10-failure-mode-screening-and-selection.mdx#questions)): **Left Abutment Cliff**, **Right
Abutment Cliff**, **Concrete Structure**, **Wall**, and an Other Location named by the text entered for it. When question 5 names no contact, DST
provides a single location, named **Location 1**, that the team names itself. The team cannot add or copy PFM-03 locations, and the locations DST creates cannot be
renamed or deleted; they follow the screening answers. Their **Location** name is read-only in the toolbar.

:::caution

Clearing a structure contact in question 5 deletes its location with its inputs, results and justifications. A contact eliminated because a cutoff
wall ties into it hides the location instead, until the tie is removed.

:::

## Settlement Against the Wall or Cliff and Failure Pathway

The **Settlement against the wall or cliff and failure pathway** section characterizes the rigid face, the crack it causes and the path that
concentrated leak erosion would follow. The information icon beside each geometry input shows a profile with that dimension highlighted.

When neither a settlement nor a Year Completed is available, the rail shows the notice "No settlement value and no year of construction", asking
for one of them. PFM-03 uses the Dam Height on the Dam Data page as the dam height.

### Readings

The readings show the values DST calculates for the location, or a dash until the system response has been calculated: **Normalized settlement**
(the maximum post-construction settlement as a percentage of the Dam Height on the Dam Data page), **Bench-to-wall ratio**, **Crack width** (mm),
**Crack depth** and **Bottom of crack** (elevation).

### Pathway figure

The drawing shows the selected cross section to scale, with the crack from the crest down to the bottom of the crack, the zones the pathway crosses,
and lines for the crest, the top of the filter, the bottom of the crack and the top of any cutoff wall. The pathway is read from the drawing on the
Dam Information page; changing the drawing changes the pathway. When no crack forms, the caption reads "No transverse crack forms against this wall
or cliff, so nothing enters the embankment through the crest."

### Node assumption cards

Below the drawing, one card per node states how DST treats it at this location: **Flaw** when the flaw probability is zero, **Initiation**,
**Filter compatibility**, **Sidewall support**, and, when they reduce the probability, **Flow limitation** and **Crack filling**. When the bench or
the foundation slope rules out a crack, the Flaw card names the cause, for example "a foundation slope of 25 degrees or less".

Inputs on this page save automatically (see [Chapter 4, Working in DST](./04-working-in-dst.mdx#saving)), the system response recalculates
automatically (see [Chapter 4, Working in DST](./04-working-in-dst.mdx#automatic-calculation)), and a change clears the location's verification (see
[Chapter 11, Failure Mode Evaluation](./11-failure-mode-evaluation.mdx#verifying-a-location)).

## System Response

The **System Response** section shows the SRP against **Headwater Elevation (ft)** on a logarithmic probability axis, as a table of up to eight headwaters
and a chart. It works as described in [Chapter 11, Failure Mode Evaluation](./11-failure-mode-evaluation.mdx#system-response).

## User-Defined SRP

With **User Defined SRP** selected, the page replaces its sections with a table of **Headwater Elevation (ft)** and **Probability** and its chart.
The table works as described in [Chapter 11, Failure Mode Evaluation](./11-failure-mode-evaluation.mdx#user-defined-srp). PFM-03 has no parameter
overrides.
