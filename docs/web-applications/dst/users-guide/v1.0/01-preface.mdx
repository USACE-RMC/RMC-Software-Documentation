# Preface

The Dam Screening Tool (DST) is a web application for screening-level risk analysis of dams. Use this User's Guide to learn how to operate DST:
creating a screening, entering its information, evaluating its potential failure modes, assembling its risk results, taking it through review, and
fixing common problems along the way. Each page of the guide covers one page of DST, with its inputs, results and messages.

The guide is written for dam safety engineers and analysts who build screenings, and for the reviewers who read and comment on them. It assumes a
working knowledge of dam safety risk analysis; terms specific to DST are defined where they first appear and in the
[glossary](./920-appendix-glossary.mdx).

The User's Guide describes how to use DST, not the engineering behind it. The calculations DST performs are documented in the DST Technical Reference
Manual and in the System Response Library manuals for each screening model.

The example screenings and dams shown in the figures are fictional, except Blue Marsh Dam, a real dam whose National Inventory of Dams (NID) data is
public.

## Document Organization

The guide follows DST's module navigation, with one section for each screening module and one page for each of the module's pages:

- **Getting started.** [Introduction](./020-introduction.mdx) describes DST and the modules of a screening.
  [Getting Started](./030-getting-started.mdx) covers accounts, signing in, the Dashboard, creating and copying screenings and the screening team.
  [Working in DST](./040-working-in-dst.mdx) covers the behaviors every screening page shares, such as saving, page messages, tables, verification,
  Submission Readiness and the input lock.
- **Screening information and hazards.** [Screening Information](./100-screening-information.mdx), [Documents](./150-documents.mdx) and
  [Hazards](./200-hazards.mdx).
- **Response.** [Response](./300-response.mdx) covers the dam information, failure mode screening, selection and evaluation. Each potential failure
  mode (PFM) has its own page, from [PFM-01](./351-pfm-01.mdx) through [PFM-23](./369-pfm-23.mdx).
- **Consequences, risk and findings.** [Consequences](./400-consequences.mdx), [Risk Analysis](./500-risk-analysis.mdx) and
  [Major Findings](./600-major-findings.mdx).
- **Review.** [Review](./700-review.mdx) covers taking a screening through review, for screening teams and reviewers.
- **References and appendices.** [References](./900-references.mdx), the [acronyms](./910-appendix-acronyms.mdx), a
  [glossary](./920-appendix-glossary.mdx) and a [quick reference to the PFM pages](./930-appendix-pfm-quick-reference.mdx).
