# Preface

The Dam Screening Tool (DST) is a web application for screening-level risk analysis of dams. This User's Guide explains how to operate DST: how to
create a screening, enter its information, evaluate its potential failure modes, assemble its risk results, and take it through review. It documents
each page, input and status indicator in the application, and the workflow that connects them.

The guide is written for dam safety engineers and analysts who build screenings, and for the reviewers who read and comment on them. It assumes a
working knowledge of dam safety risk analysis; terms specific to the DST interface are defined where they first appear.

The User's Guide describes how to use DST, not the engineering behind it. The calculations DST performs are documented in the DST Technical Reference
Manual and in the System Response Library manuals for each screening model.

The example screenings and dams shown in the figures are fictional, except Blue Marsh Dam, a real dam whose National Inventory of Dams (NID) data is
public.

## Document Organization

- **Getting started.** [Chapter 2, Introduction](./02-introduction.mdx), describes what DST is and the modules of a screening.
  [Chapter 3, Getting Started](./03-getting-started.mdx), covers accounts, signing in, the Dashboard, creating and copying screenings, the screening
  team and the Profile page. [Chapter 4, Working in DST](./04-working-in-dst.mdx), covers the navigation and the behaviors shared by every screening
  page: saving, automatic calculation, page messages, tables, the summary tray, verification, Submission Readiness, the input lock and editing
  conflicts.
- **Screening information and hazards.** [Chapter 5, Screening Information](./05-screening-information.mdx), covers the Overview, Dam Data,
  Operational Data, Screening Management and Screening Activity pages. [Chapter 6, Documents](./06-documents.mdx), covers the screening's document
  library. [Chapter 7, Hazards](./07-hazards.mdx), covers the Flood Hazard, Stage Duration and Seismic Hazard pages.
- **Response.** [Chapter 8, Dam Information](./08-dam-information.mdx), and
  [Chapter 9, Cross-Section Editors](./09-cross-section-editors.mdx), describe the dam's materials and cross sections.
  [Chapter 10, Failure Mode Screening and Selection](./10-failure-mode-screening-and-selection.mdx), describes choosing the potential failure modes
  to evaluate. [Chapter 11, Failure Mode Evaluation](./11-failure-mode-evaluation.mdx), describes the evaluation page and the parts every failure
  mode page shares. Chapters 12 through 30 each document one potential failure mode (PFM) page, from PFM-01 through PFM-23.
- **Consequences, risk and findings.** [Chapter 31, Consequences](./31-consequences.mdx), covers the inundation and consequence pages.
  [Chapter 32, Risk Analysis](./32-risk-analysis.mdx), covers the Setup and Results pages.
  [Chapter 33, Major Findings](./33-major-findings.mdx), covers the screening team's findings and recommendations.
- **Review.** [Chapter 34, Review](./34-review.mdx), describes taking a screening through review, for screening teams and reviewers.
- **References and appendices.** [Chapter 35, References](./35-references.mdx), lists the works cited. The appendices give the
  [acronyms](./36-appendix-acronyms.mdx), a [glossary](./37-appendix-glossary.mdx) and a
  [quick reference to the PFM pages](./38-appendix-pfm-quick-reference.mdx).
