# Preface

The U.S. Army Corps of Engineers (USACE) Risk Management Center (RMC) Dam Screening Tool (DST) is a web application for screening-level risk analysis
of dams. In DST, a screening team documents a dam, evaluates its potential failure modes, estimates the consequences of failure and the resulting
risk, and takes the screening through review.

The main purpose of DST is to help a screening team estimate the risk posed by any dam from the information available for that dam and the team's
judgment. DST keeps a dam's inputs, results, findings and review history together in one screening, so that the basis for each risk estimate is
documented and can be reviewed.

The purpose of the User's Guide is to provide users with information on getting started with DST, the inputs each page requires and how to enter
them, the results and messages each page reports, and solutions to common problems.
