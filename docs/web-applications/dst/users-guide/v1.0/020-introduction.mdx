# Introduction

This page describes the purpose of DST, the two types of screening and the module that holds each part of the work.

The Dam Screening Tool (DST) is a web application for screening-level risk analysis of dams. A screening team uses DST to characterize the hazards
loading a dam, identify and evaluate the dam's potential failure modes, estimate the consequences of failure, and combine those pieces into risk
estimates. The team then records its findings and takes the screening through review, all within the same application.

All of a dam's work in DST is organized as a **screening**. A screening holds everything the team enters, every result DST produces for that dam, and
the screening's review history. Each screening has a team led by a Facilitator, and the team can work on the screening's pages at the same time.

## Screening-Level Risk Analysis

A screening-level risk analysis estimates a dam's risk from the information readily available for the dam and the judgment of the screening team. DST
recommends which potential failure modes to evaluate, guides the estimate of the system response for each one, and runs the hydraulic, consequence and
risk computations.

DST reports risk estimates; it does not make dam safety decisions, and its results are not a substitute for a detailed risk assessment. How DST
computes each result is documented in the DST Technical Reference Manual and in the System Response Library manuals for each screening model.

DST supports two types of screening:

- An **Official** screening is a screening of record that can be submitted for review.
- A **Testing** screening is for practice and training; it cannot be submitted for review or produce official reports.

The modules are not a strict sequence. The team may work in any module at any time, and several team members may work in different modules at once.
Results on most pages calculate automatically when their inputs change; the Consequences module's HEC-RAS and LifeSim runs are the exception and
are started by the user (see [Consequences](./400-consequences.mdx)). A team member verifies the data on each page once it is complete.
[Working in DST](./040-working-in-dst.mdx) describes these shared behaviors.

## Using This Guide

[Getting Started](./030-getting-started.mdx) explains how to sign in and create a screening, and [Working in DST](./040-working-in-dst.mdx) describes
the behaviors every screening page shares. Help for each page of DST is in the guide page of the same name, listed under its module in the sidebar.

The guide names pages, buttons and fields exactly as they appear in DST, in **bold** where the user selects or enters them.
