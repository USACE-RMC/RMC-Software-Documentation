# Consequence Data

Use the Consequence Data page, at **Consequences > Consequence Data**, to add the structure inventory, rate the Emergency Response of the model area
and, optionally, mark a non-failure hazard zone. The consequence simulations on the Consequence Analysis page need a structure inventory and a rated
(or Unknown) Emergency Response.

## Structure inventory

**Structures** opens the **Add Structure Inventory** dialog, with two tabs:

- **National Structure Inventory** downloads the USACE National Structure Inventory (NSI) structures inside the model area. It needs a model area
  with a drawn outline.
- **Upload File** reads a GeoJSON FeatureCollection of points with NSI attribute names, up to 2 GB, chosen with **Select GeoJSON File**.

**Name** is optional; a blank name is generated. DST reports "Structure inventory "[name]" is ready ([N] structures)." when it finishes.

:::caution

Replacing an inventory deletes the old one, with any map edits to its structures, once the new one is read.

:::

The inventory's properties panel shows its details (source, file, import date, number of structures and the structures edited since the import,
listed by **View Edits**), and two cards:

- **LifeSim checks** counts the structures with a problem, such as "Outside the Emergency Response area", "Missing population", "Missing occupancy
  type", "Missing foundation height" or "More people than the occupancy holds". **Show** colors those structures by **LifeSim data checks** and zooms
  to them (see [Structure symbology](./401-inundation-geometry.mdx#structure-symbology)). These checks are warnings and do not stop a run, but
  LifeSim leaves out structures outside the Emergency Response area.
- **Summary** gives the day and night population, under 65 and 65 and over, the total value, and the structures counted by a characteristic chosen
  in **Structures by** (damage category by default).

## Editing structures

Use **Edit Structures**, or **Edit selected feature** in the map toolbar, to correct the inventory on the map: move points, add points with **Add
points**, and change a point's attributes in the panel. **Undo** reverses the last change, and **Save** or **Cancel** ends the edit. A new point
starts with the nearest structure's attributes. <TableReference tableKey="dst-users-guide-table-consequences-structure" /> lists a structure's
fields.

## Emergency Response

The Emergency Response rates how well the people in the model area would be warned and would evacuate. It always exists, and its area is the model
area. <TableReference tableKey="dst-users-guide-table-consequences-emergency-response" /> lists its fields. The ratings save when chosen, and the
notes when the user leaves the box.

## Non-failure hazard zone

The non-failure hazard zone is the part of the model area that floods even if the dam does not fail. On the Consequence Analysis page it gets its own
hazard occurrence and advanced notice. A screening has at most one. **Non-Failure Zone** opens the **Add Non-Failure Hazard Zone** dialog, with three
tabs:

- **Draw on Map**: **Start Drawing**, then a click at each corner; a double-click or Enter finishes.
- **Import a File**: a GeoJSON file of Polygon or MultiPolygon features in longitude and latitude (WGS84), up to 50 MB and 200,000 points. All its
  polygons become one zone, and overlapping polygons are merged. An outline with more than 5,000 points is simplified when it is saved.
- **Inundation Result**: the wet cells of one **Loading level**'s maximum depth grid, widened by a **Buffer distance** (100 ft by default, from 0 to
  5,000 ft; 30 m by default in a metric screening, up to 1,524 m) and cut to the model area.

:::tip

A zone that matches the area a non-failure flood reaches comes from running a non-failure analysis on the Inundation Analysis page first, then
making the zone from one of its loading levels on the **Inundation Result** tab.

:::

After a zone is made from an inundation result, a message says what was done, for example "The part outside the model area was cut off.", "The
outline was simplified from [N] to [M] points.", "[N] holes smaller than [area] were filled in." and "[N] separate pieces smaller than [area] were
left out."

The zone's properties panel shows its area and source. **Edit Geometry** reshapes an outline of one piece without holes; any other outline is changed
by replacing the zone. Deleting the zone also removes its hazard occurrence and advanced notice.

## Common problems

- **The NSI download is unavailable.** The tab reads "No model areas with geometry found. Draw or upload at least one model area polygon before
  downloading structures from NSI." The user draws the model area on the Inundation Geometry page first.
- **An inventory fails to load.** DST reports "The NSI download failed." or "The structure inventory upload failed.", with the reason. The user checks that an
  uploaded file is a GeoJSON FeatureCollection of points with NSI attribute names, and tries again. When the National Structure Inventory service is
  down or degraded, the tab shows the outage's site alert (see [Site alerts](./040-working-in-dst.mdx#site-alerts)); the download can still be
  started, but may fail until the service recovers.
- **A zone file is refused.** The **Import a File** tab gives the reason: "The file is [size], larger than the 50.0 MB that can be imported.
  Simplify its outline and import it again.", "The file's polygons have [N] points, more than the 200,000 that can be imported. Simplify the file and
  import it again." or "The file couldn't be read."
- **The Inundation Result tab has nothing to use.** It reads "No loading level has a finished maximum depth grid yet. Run an analysis on the
  Inundation Analysis tab first."
- **Structures are left out of the simulation.** LifeSim leaves out structures outside the Emergency Response area. **Show** on the **LifeSim checks**
  card finds them; the user then moves them or enlarges the model area.
