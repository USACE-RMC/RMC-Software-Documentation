# Consequence Data

The Consequence Data page, at **Consequences > Consequence Data**, holds the inputs of the consequence simulations: the structure inventory, the
Emergency Response ratings of the model area, and an optional non-failure hazard zone.

The page is a map (see [The map](./401-inundation-geometry.mdx#the-map)). The **Consequence Data** panel at the upper left has two add buttons, **Structures** and **Non-Failure
Zone**, and lists the structure inventory, **Emergency Response** and **Non-Failure Hazard Zone**. Selecting an item opens its properties at the
right. A row marked incomplete gives the reason, for example "Incomplete: Draw its outline on the map, or delete it."

## Structure inventory

**Structures** opens the **Add Structure Inventory** dialog, with two tabs:

- **National Structure Inventory** downloads the USACE National Structure Inventory (NSI) structures inside the model area. It needs a model area.
- **Upload File** reads a GeoJSON FeatureCollection of points with NSI attribute names, up to 2 GB, chosen with **Select GeoJSON File**.

**Name** is optional; a blank name is generated. Replacing an inventory deletes the old one and any map edits to its structures once the new one is
read. DST reports "Structure inventory "[name]" is ready ([N] structures)." when it finishes.

The inventory's properties panel shows its **Name**, its details (source, file, import date, number of structures and the structures edited since
the import, listed by **View Edits**), and two cards:

- **LifeSim checks** counts the structures with a problem, such as "Outside the Emergency Response area", "Missing population", "Missing occupancy
  type", "Missing foundation height" or "More people than the occupancy holds". **Show** colors those structures and zooms to them. These are
  warnings; they do not stop a run, though LifeSim leaves out structures outside the Emergency Response area.
- **Summary** gives the day and night population, under 65 and 65 and over, the total value, and the structures counted by a characteristic chosen
  in **Structures by** (damage category by default).

## Editing structures

**Edit Structures**, or **Edit selected feature** in the toolbar, edits the inventory's structures on the map: moving points, adding points with
**Add points**, and changing a point's attributes in the panel at the right. **Undo** reverses the last change, and **Save** or **Cancel** ends the
edit. A new point starts with the nearest structure's attributes. <TableReference tableKey="dst-users-guide-table-consequences-structure" /> lists a
structure's fields.

## Emergency Response

The Emergency Response rates how well the people in the model area would be warned and would evacuate. It always exists, and its area is the model
area. <TableReference tableKey="dst-users-guide-table-consequences-emergency-response" /> lists its fields. The ratings save when chosen, and the
notes when the user leaves the box.

## Non-failure hazard zone

The non-failure hazard zone is the part of the model area that floods even if the dam does not fail. On the Consequence Analysis page it gets its own
hazard occurrence and advanced notice. A screening has at most one. **Non-Failure Zone** opens the **Add Non-Failure Hazard Zone** dialog, with three
tabs:

- **Draw on Map**: **Start Drawing**, then a click at each corner; a double-click or Enter finishes.
- **Import a File**: a GeoJSON file of polygons in longitude and latitude (WGS 84), up to 50 MB and 200,000 points. All its polygons become one zone.
- **Inundation Result**: the wet cells of one **Loading level**'s maximum depth grid, widened by a **Buffer distance** (100 ft by default, from 0 to
  5,000 ft) and cut to the model area.

The zone's properties panel shows its area and source. **Edit Geometry** reshapes an outline of one piece without holes; any other outline is changed
by replacing the zone. Deleting the zone also removes its hazard occurrence and advanced notice.
