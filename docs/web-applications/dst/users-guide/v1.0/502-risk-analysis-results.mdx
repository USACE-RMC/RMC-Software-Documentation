# Results

The Results page, at **Risk Analysis > Results**, shows the risk of the screening. It has no inputs; its toggles only change the view. While the risk
computes, the page reads "Computing risk results…", and values not yet computed show "—".

## Excess Risk Summary

Four tiles summarize the excess risk: **Total APF** (the adjusted annual probability of failure), **Total EALL** (the expected annual life loss),
**Total EAD** (the expected annual damages) and **Individual Risk**, which is the total APF times a fatality rate. The fatality rate is the highest
fatality rate of the failure functions in use, from the loading level or table row it comes from, or 71%, the LifeSim average fatality rate for a high
hazard individual, when none of the functions has one.

## Societal Excess Life Safety Risk

This section shows the life safety risk of failure, above the background risk:

- **Mean Risk Plot for Excess Societal Risk** plots each failure mode and the **Total (adjusted)** as APF against expected life loss given failure.
  **TRG line** (on by default) shows the EALL = 1E-03 line, and **Adjusted PFM values** (off by default) plots the failure modes' adjusted values in
  place of their marginal ones.
- **Loss Exceedance Curve for Expected Annual Life Loss** plots the annual exceedance probability of each excess life loss.
- **Risk Contribution** lists the share of the adjusted total APF and EALL from each failure mode, the top eight and then "All others". Resting the
  pointer on a row highlights its series on the plots.

## Societal Excess Economic Risk

This section shows the same views for damages: the **Mean Risk Plot for Excess Economic Risk**, with expected damages given failure, the **Loss
Exceedance Curve for Expected Annual Damages**, and the failure modes' **Risk Contribution** to EAD.

## Total Risk

This section adds the background risk from the non-failure function to the excess risk. The **Loss Exceedance Curves for Annual Life Loss** and
**Loss Exceedance Curves for Annual Damages** plot the **Excess**, **Background** and **Total** curves.

<TableReference tableKey="dst-users-guide-table-risk-components" /> lists the rows of the risk components table, which gives each as life (EALL) and
economic (EAD) risk. **Risk Contribution** bars show the excess and background risk as shares of the total.
