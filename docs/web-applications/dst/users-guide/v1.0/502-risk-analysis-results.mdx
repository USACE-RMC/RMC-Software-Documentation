# Results

The Results page (**Risk Analysis > Results**) reports the risk of the screening: the summary values, the life safety and economic risk of each
failure mode, and the total risk including the background risk. The page has no inputs; its toggles change only the view. While the risk
computes, the page reads "Computing risk results…", and values not yet computed show "—".

## Excess Risk Summary

Four tiles summarize the excess risk: **Total APF** (the adjusted annual probability of failure), **Total EALL** (the expected annual life loss),
**Total EAD** (the expected annual damages) and **Individual Risk**, the total APF times a fatality rate.

The info button on the **Individual Risk** tile shows the calculation with the screening's values, "[total APF] × [fatality rate] = [individual
risk]", and where the fatality rate comes from. The rate is the highest fatality rate among the failure functions assigned on the Setup page: for each
loading level, the highest mean fatality rate of any structure in its LifeSim results, and any rate entered on the Results Data page. When none of
those functions has a rate, DST uses 71%, the LifeSim average fatality rate for a high hazard individual.

## Societal Excess Life Safety Risk

This section shows the life safety risk of failure, above the background risk:

- **Mean Risk Plot for Excess Societal Risk** plots each failure mode and the **Total (adjusted)** as APF against expected life loss given failure.
  A failure mode with locations is one point, named with its number followed by "(composite)".
- **Loss Exceedance Curve for Expected Annual Life Loss** plots the annual exceedance probability of each excess life loss.
- **Risk Contribution** lists each failure mode's share of the adjusted total APF and EALL, the top eight and then "All others". Below the rows,
  notes list the failure modes that are not plotted, with the reason.

The two toggles above the plots change the view only:

- **TRG line** (on by default) draws the tolerable risk guideline, labeled "EALL = 1E-03", on both life safety plots.
- **Adjusted PFM values** (off by default) plots the failure modes' combination-adjusted values in place of their marginal values, on every plot of
  the page. The **Total (adjusted)** and the **Risk Contribution** panels always show adjusted values.

:::tip

Resting the pointer on a failure mode's point, curve or **Risk Contribution** row highlights that failure mode on every plot of the page.

:::

## Societal Excess Economic Risk

This section shows the same views for damages: the **Mean Risk Plot for Excess Economic Risk**, with expected damages given failure, the **Loss
Exceedance Curve for Expected Annual Damages**, and the failure modes' **Risk Contribution** to EAD. The economic plots have no guideline line.

## Total Risk

This section adds the background risk from the non-failure function to the excess risk. The **Loss Exceedance Curves for Annual Life Loss** and
**Loss Exceedance Curves for Annual Damages** plot the **Excess**, **Background** and **Total** curves.
<TableReference tableKey="dst-users-guide-table-risk-components" /> lists the rows of the risk components table, which gives each as life (EALL) and
economic (EAD) risk. **Risk Contribution** bars show the excess and background risk as shares of the total.
