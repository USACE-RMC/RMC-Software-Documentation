# PFM-07: CLE due to Poor Compaction Along Foundation or Abutment

Potential failure mode 07 (PFM-07) is concentrated leak erosion (CLE) through poorly compacted embankment fill along its contact with the foundation
or an abutment. The flaw runs along the contact, and the reservoir (headwater) loads it whenever the pool rises above the flaw. The PFM-07 page
characterizes the contact and the compaction against it, reads the seepage pathway from an embankment cross section, and produces the system
response probability (SRP) curve for each location. The parts of the page shared with every potential failure mode page are described in
[Chapter 11, Failure Mode Evaluation](./11-failure-mode-evaluation.mdx#the-failure-mode-page).

The user opens a PFM-07 location from the Failure Mode Evaluation page (**Response > Failure Mode Evaluation**, then the location under PFM-07; see
[Opening a location](./11-failure-mode-evaluation.mdx#opening-a-location)). The page heading reads **07 - CLE due to Poor Compaction Along Foundation
or Abutment**. <FigReference figKey="dst-users-guide-fig-pfm-07-page" /> shows the page, and
<TableReference tableKey="dst-users-guide-table-pfm-07-regions" /> describes each numbered region.

## Contact Flaw and Seepage Pathway

The **Contact flaw and seepage pathway** section describes the contact and how the fill against it was compacted, and shows the pathway DST reads
from the cross section. The section follows the characterization pattern described in
[Characterization](./11-failure-mode-evaluation.mdx#characterization). On a wide screen the inputs, readings and pathway figure sit side by side; on
a narrower screen the readings move to a band below them.

them. The compaction drop-down is labeled **Foundation contact zone compaction** or **Abutment contact zone compaction** to match the contact, and

### Pathway figure

The pathway figure draws the selected cross section to scale with its material zones, a legend of the materials present, and a shaded band for the
headwater analysis range. A line labeled "Failure pathway" follows the flaw through the section and ends at the first drain or filter it meets; the
legend shows whether the pathway meets a filter, and marks a cutoff wall or upstream facing when the Dam Information page has one. Callouts at the
side give elevations such as the crest and the base of the flaw.

Until DST resolves the pathway, the caption reads "The pathway is read from the drawing once the model has run." followed by the calculation state;
afterwards it reads "Drawn to scale from the selected cross section." with the elevation units. Without a selected cross section the figure shows a
placeholder. The cross-section figure is described in [Characterization](./11-failure-mode-evaluation.mdx#characterization).

### Node assumption cards

A row of cards under the pathway figure states what DST assumes at each node of the PFM-07 event tree, from the inputs and the current results:

- **Flaw** appears only for a well-prepared contact, and reads, for example, "For a compacted soil foundation, the probability of a continuous flaw is
  zero." or "For a regular or treated rock foundation surface, the probability of a continuous flaw is zero." ("abutment" replaces "foundation" for an
  abutment contact).
- **Initiation** states that initiation is evaluated for pools above the failure pathway, and whether the critical shear stress and the initial
  pipe diameter come from defaults or from entered distributions.
- **Filter compatibility** names the drain or filter the pathway reaches and the materials evaluated against it, or reports that the pathway reaches
  no filter.
- **Roof support** names the pathway materials that can hold up the roof of an eroding pipe.
- **Flow limitation** appears only when an upstream element, such as a cutoff wall, limits flow, and describes it.
- **Crack filling** appears only when shell material may fill the flaw.

### Advanced initiation overrides

The **Advanced initiation overrides** bar opens a tray with its description "Screening defaults come from the governing initiation material and stay
locked until a site-specific distribution is chosen." The bar counts the overridden parameters and those that still need a justification.
<TableReference tableKey="dst-users-guide-table-pfm-07-overrides" /> lists the two parameters. Each card's drop-down starts at **Default** with the
default distribution; choosing **Deterministic**, **Uniform**, **Triangular** or **PERT** unlocks its parameter boxes. **Revert to defaults** returns
both parameters to their defaults. Override trays, distribution cards and their justifications are described in
[Parameter overrides](./11-failure-mode-evaluation.mdx#parameter-overrides).

## System Response

The **System Response** section shows the PFM-07 SRP curve, its table and the eight nodal tables, as described in
[System Response](./11-failure-mode-evaluation.mdx#system-response), [SRP charts](./11-failure-mode-evaluation.mdx#srp-charts) and
[Nodal tables](./11-failure-mode-evaluation.mdx#nodal-tables). The chart plots **System Response Probability** on a logarithmic axis from 1E-6
against **Headwater Elevation (ft)**, and **Download SRP curves as a CSV file** exports the curves.
<TableReference tableKey="dst-users-guide-table-pfm-07-nodes" /> lists the nodes. A pool-independent node takes one value for every headwater.
Revising a node is described in [Nodal revisions](./11-failure-mode-evaluation.mdx#nodal-revisions), and a notice about out-of-date results in
[Calculation currency](./11-failure-mode-evaluation.mdx#calculation-currency).

## User-Defined SRP

With **User Defined SRP** selected, the characterization and system response sections are replaced by a grid of headwater elevations and
probabilities and its chart, with **Headwater Elevation (ft)** on the x axis. The grid and the switch between modes are described in
[User-defined SRP](./11-failure-mode-evaluation.mdx#user-defined-srp) and [Active SRP](./11-failure-mode-evaluation.mdx#active-srp).
