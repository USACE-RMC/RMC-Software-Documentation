# PFM-02: CLE due to Cross Valley Differential Settlement

Potential failure mode 02 (PFM-02) evaluates concentrated leak erosion (CLE) through a transverse crack at the crest, where the embankment settles
differentially across the valley at a steep abutment slope or bench. The loading is the reservoir pool, and the page plots the system response against
headwater elevation. [Failure Mode Pages](./342-failure-mode-pages.mdx) describes the parts of the page that every failure mode page shares.

## Location and Cross Section

DST creates the PFM-02 locations from the abutment slopes and benches entered on the Dam Information page (see
[Abutment Slopes](./310-dam-information.mdx#abutment-slopes)):

- one location for each abutment whose top slope is between 15° and 65°, named **Left Abutment** or **Right Abutment**;
- one location for every bench on either abutment, named for example **Left Abutment Bench 1**.

An abutment into which a cutoff wall reaching the crest ties (Failure Mode Screening question 7a) has no locations. When no abutment yields a
location, DST provides a single location, named **Location 1**, that the team describes itself, with its own abutment slope. The team cannot add or
copy PFM-02 locations, and the locations DST creates cannot be renamed or deleted; they follow the Dam Information page and the screening answers.
Their **Location** name is read-only in the toolbar.

:::caution

When an abutment's top slope leaves the range 15° to 65°, or a bench is removed, on the Dam Information page, DST deletes that location with its
inputs, results and justifications. A location dropped because a cutoff wall now ties into its abutment (question 7a) is hidden instead, and returns
with its inputs when the tie is removed.

:::

## Cross-Valley Settlement and Failure Pathway

The **Cross-valley settlement and failure pathway** section characterizes the crack and the path that concentrated leak erosion would follow
through the embankment. The inputs rail holds the values the team supplies, and the readings and drawing show what DST derives from them.

When neither a settlement nor a Year Completed is available, the rail shows the notice "No settlement value and no year of construction", asking
for one of them.

### Readings

The readings show the values DST calculates for the location, or a dash until the system response has been calculated:
**Normalized settlement** (the maximum post-construction settlement as a percentage of the dam height at the point of maximum settlement), the slope
(**Top abutment slope**, **Slope below Bench N** or **Abutment slope**, from the Dam Information page or this page), **Localized tensile strain**,
**Crack width** (mm), **Crack depth** and **Bottom of crack** (elevation). PFM-02 uses the Dam Height on the Dam Data page as the dam height for
every location.

### Pathway figure

The drawing shows the selected cross section to scale, with the crack from the crest down to the bottom of the crack and the zones the pathway
crosses, and lines for the crest, the top of the filter, the bottom of the crack and the top of any cutoff wall. The pathway is read from the
drawing on the Dam Information page; changing the drawing changes the pathway. Until DST has calculated, the caption reads that the pathway is read
from the drawing once the model has run. When no crack forms, it reads "No transverse crack forms at this settlement, so nothing enters the
embankment through the crest."

### Node assumption cards

Below the drawing, one card per node states how DST treats it at this location: **Flaw** (when continuous cracking is observed), **Initiation**,
**Filter compatibility**, **Sidewall support**, and, when they reduce the probability, **Flow limitation** and **Crack filling**. For example, the
Initiation card reads "Initiation is assumed to occur for all pools that exceed the bottom of the crack elevation. A probability of zero is assigned
for all pools below the bottom of the crack."

Inputs on this page save automatically (see [Saving](./040-working-in-dst.mdx#saving)), the system response recalculates
automatically (see [Automatic Calculation](./040-working-in-dst.mdx#automatic-calculation)), and a change clears the location's verification (see
[Verifying a location](./342-failure-mode-pages.mdx#verifying-a-location)).

## User-Defined SRP

With **User Defined SRP** selected, the page replaces its sections with a table of **Headwater Elevation (ft)** and **Probability** and its chart.
The table works as described in [User-defined SRP](./343-system-response.mdx#user-defined-srp).

## Failure Mode Summary

The **Failure mode summary** tray lists the screening answers this location reads that were answered Unknown (see
[Failure mode summary](./342-failure-mode-pages.mdx#failure-mode-summary)). For PFM-02, an Unknown cracking cause is treated as
settlement cracking. PFM-02 has no parameter overrides.
