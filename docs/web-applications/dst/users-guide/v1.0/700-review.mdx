# Review

After the Facilitator submits a screening, it passes through its review levels one at a time. At each level, the reviewers comment, the screening
team responds to each comment, the reviewers close or return each comment, and the review lead approves the level. When the last level is approved,
the screening is Complete. The review levels and their reviewers are set up in the Review Configuration section of the Screening Management page (see
[Screening Management](./104-screening-management.mdx)).

Everyone assigned to a screening can open the Review module and read every level's comments. Reviewers can read every page of the screening, but
cannot change its inputs. Selecting **Review** in the module navigation opens:

- the level's page, for a reviewer on a level that is in progress;
- the **Submission Readiness** page, for the Facilitator, Team Members and administrators before submission;
- the **Review Levels** page, for everyone else.

## The Review Lifecycle

A review level passes through five phases. Its phase appears as a badge on the Review Levels page and on the Dashboard's My Reviews tab (see
[My Reviews](./030-getting-started.mdx#my-reviews)). <TableReference tableKey="dst-users-guide-table-review-phases" /> lists the phases in order and
whether the screening's inputs can be changed in each.

The first level starts its review period as soon as the screening is submitted. Each later level starts its review period as soon as the level
before it is approved, and its reviewers are notified. While inputs are locked, a strip across the top of every page explains why (see
[Input Lock](./040-working-in-dst.mdx#input-lock)).

Resolution and backcheck repeat in rounds until every comment is closed, and the level's page shows the round number. A review lead can reject the
review at any time from the review period through backcheck, which ends the review of the whole screening, not only of that level (see
[Rejecting a Review](#rejecting-a-review)).

## Submitting for Review

The Facilitator submits the screening from **Review > Submission Readiness** once every page tile reads Ready. Submitting starts the first level's
review period, notifies its reviewers and locks the screening's inputs. A submission cannot be withdrawn except by a rejection. The steps, the
readiness statuses and the confirmation dialog are described in [Submitting for Review](./040-working-in-dst.mdx#submitting-for-review). When
**Submit for Review** stays unavailable, see [Submit for Review is unavailable](#problem-submit-for-review-is-unavailable).

(Prior Cycles)** section below the table.

### The Review Team

:::note[Availability]

The Facilitator, administrators and the level's review lead.

:::

**Review Team** opens a dialog listing the level's reviewers, the review lead marked **(Lead)**. During and after the review period, each reviewer
has a participation status:

- **Pending**: the reviewer has not yet commented or submitted No Comments.
- **Participated**: the reviewer has commented or submitted No Comments. The review lead counts as Participated when the review period ends.
- **Unresponsive**: the reviewer did not participate.

Once the review period starts, the button shows the participation count, for example **Review Team (1/2)**; on a Pending level it shows the number of
reviewers, such as **Review Team (2)**. In the dialog:

- **Adding and removing reviewers.** While the level is Pending or in its review period, the search box adds a reviewer, and the trash icon beside a
  reviewer removes the reviewer. A reviewer added during the review period is notified and can comment at once. The review lead cannot be removed
  here; only the Facilitator or an administrator assigns the review lead, on the Screening Management page. During resolution and backcheck the team
  cannot be changed.
- **Marking Unresponsive.** During the active phases the review lead selects **Mark Unresponsive** beside a Pending reviewer, enters a
  **Justification (required)** and selects **Confirm**. **Remove Unresponsive** reverses it, except during backcheck. When the review lead ends the
  review period, every reviewer still Pending is marked Unresponsive.

### Importing and Exporting Comments

**Export**, available to everyone who can open the level, downloads comments as an Excel workbook (.xlsx). In the **Export Comments** dialog, the user
checks the levels to include, or **Select all**, and selects **Export N Levels**. The workbook has one worksheet per level, and one row per comment of
the current review cycle with the columns **Comment #**, **Category**, **Commenter**, **Comment Text**, **Critical**, **Status**, **Disposition**,
**Response Text** and **Backcheck Text**. The last three hold only the latest team response and the latest backcheck reply; earlier replies in a
thread and archived comments are not exported. The file is named for the screening, the level and the date, for example
`Screening-Name_Level-1_comments_2026-11-20.xlsx`.

**Import** adds many comments at once.

:::note[Availability]

The level's reviewers, during the review and resolution periods.

:::

DST checks every row when the rows are read and again after each edit in the preview:

- **Category** must name one of the seven modules or General, in any capitalization. Otherwise the row has the error 'Invalid category: "[value]".'
- **Sub-category** is optional. When given, it must name a page of the row's category, or the row has the error '"[page]" is not a sub-category of
  [category].'
- **Comment** is required ("Comment text is required.") and holds at most 5,000 characters ("Comment text exceeds the 5000-character limit.").
- **Critical** takes Yes or No. A blank cell is taken as No with the warning "Critical defaulted to No.", and any other value is taken as No with the
  warning 'Unrecognized critical value: "[value]". Defaulted to No.'

Rows with warnings are imported; rows with errors must be corrected or deleted first. When the first row has Category and Comment headings, DST reads
the columns by their headings rather than by their order. Blank rows are skipped, an import holds at most 5,000 rows, and only the first worksheet of
an uploaded workbook is read. Imported comments count as the reviewer's participation. A reviewer who has submitted No Comments must revert it before
importing.

### No Comments

:::note[Availability]

The level's reviewers, during the review or resolution period. Not offered to a reviewer who already has comments on the level.

:::

A reviewer with nothing to raise selects **No Comments** on the level's page. The "Submit 'No Comments'" dialog explains that the reviewer is
attesting to having no comments for the level and cannot add regular comments without reverting the entry; **Submit No Comments** confirms. The entry
appears in the table with the status Concurrence and counts as participation. During the review period, **Revert 'No Comments'** removes the entry.

### Editing and Deleting a Comment

During the review period, the author of a comment sees **Edit** and **Delete** in its panel. **Edit** opens the comment's Category, Sub-category,
Comment and **Mark as critical** for change; **Save changes** keeps the change and **Cancel** discards it. **Delete** asks for confirmation.

:::caution

Deleting a comment permanently removes it from the review record ("It cannot be undone."). Only the author can delete a comment, and only during the
review period.

:::

### Ending the Review Period

The review lead ends the review period with **End Review Period**, on the Review Levels page or on the level's page. The confirmation dialog states
that reviewers can no longer submit or revise comments, that the screening team can begin responding, and which reviewers will be marked Unresponsive
because they have not participated. Selecting **End Review Period** in the dialog moves the level to its resolution period, unlocks the screening's
inputs and notifies the screening team.

### Submitting for Backcheck

:::note[Requires]

A response to every comment, and a screening that passes Submission Readiness.

:::

The Facilitator selects **Submit for Backcheck** in the strip. The confirmation dialog states that screening inputs lock until the reviewers finish,
that the level's reviewers are notified to close or return each of their comments, and that inputs unlock for another resolution round if any comment
is returned. Selecting **Submit for Backcheck** in the dialog starts the backcheck period. When the button is unavailable, resting the pointer on it
shows why (see [Submit for Backcheck is unavailable](#problem-submit-for-backcheck-is-unavailable)).

## Backchecking Responses

During the backcheck period, each reviewer checks the team's responses to the reviewer's own comments. The strip above the level reads, for example,
"Backcheck — round 1" and "2 comments awaiting backcheck". The author of a Responded comment sees a reply form below the thread
(<FigReference figKey="dst-users-guide-fig-review-comment-backcheck" />).

<Figure
  figKey="dst-users-guide-fig-review-comment-backcheck"
  src="figures/web-applications/dst/users-guide/v1.0/figures/review-comment-backcheck.png"
  alt="The comment panel for Comment #2 during the first backcheck round, signed in as the reviewer who wrote it. The tags read Hazards, Flood Hazard, Critical and Responded, above the comment text and its byline. The Team Response shows a Concur tag, 'by Jordan Hale 11/20/2026, 8:20:03 AM' and the response 'We will extend the flood hazard curve to 822 ft using the most remote floods and update the hazard page.'. Below it, the empty reply box 'Your response (optional to close, required to return the comment to the team)' is followed by Return to team and Close with response, both unavailable, and Close without response."
  caption="Backchecking a team response."
  width="50%"
/>

The reviewer opens each Responded comment and chooses one of three actions:

- **Close without response** closes the comment. The thread ends with a **Closed without response** entry.
- **Close with response** records the text in the reply box as a Backcheck reply and closes the comment.
- **Return to team** records the reply and returns the comment to the team with the status Open.

The two actions that record a reply stay unavailable until the reply box holds text. New comments cannot be added during backcheck; to raise a further
request, the reviewer returns the original comment with a reply. When no comment is left awaiting backcheck, DST ends the round:

- If any comment was returned, the level goes back to its resolution period for the next round, the inputs unlock and the screening team is
  notified.
- If every comment is closed, the strip reads "All comments closed — awaiting the review lead's approval" and the review lead is notified.

**Reopen** reopens a closed comment. Reopening returns the comment to Responded when the team has responded, and to Open otherwise.

:::note[Availability]

The comment's author or the review lead, and only during the phase in which the comment was closed.

:::

### Review Lead Actions

:::note[Availability]

The review lead, on another reviewer's comment.

:::

The **Review Lead Actions** box is used when a reviewer cannot act:

- **Force Close**, while the comment is Open or Responded, closes it. The lead enters a **Force close justification (required for the record)** and
  selects **Confirm Force Close**. The justification appears in the comment's Closed entry.
- **Backcheck on Behalf**, during backcheck, replies for the author. The lead enters a **Backcheck comment** and a **Lead justification (required for
  the record)** and selects **Submit Backcheck**, which returns the comment to the team.

## Approving or Rejecting a Level

:::note[Availability]

The review lead, during the level's backcheck period.

:::

The **Official Review Decision** panel appears above the level's heading (<FigReference figKey="dst-users-guide-fig-review-decision-panel" />). It
summarizes the reviewers accounted for and the comments closed, and lists the **Approval Criteria**, each marked met or not met.

<Figure
  figKey="dst-users-guide-fig-review-decision-panel"
  src="figures/web-applications/dst/users-guide/v1.0/figures/review-decision-panel.png"
  alt="The Official Review Decision panel for Level 1, signed in as the Level 1 review lead. The summary reads Reviewers, '2 / 2 accounted for', and Comments, '4 / 4 closed'. The Approval Criteria list shows three met criteria: 'All reviewers accounted for (2 participated or marked unresponsive)', 'All 4 comments closed' and 'Screening passes Submission Readiness'. The Approve and Reject buttons follow."
  caption="The Official Review Decision panel."
  width="100%"
/>

**Approve** is available only when all three criteria are met:

- "All reviewers accounted for (N participated or marked unresponsive)". While a reviewer is still Pending it reads "N of M reviewers have not
  participated".
- "All N comments closed", or "No comments were submitted". While comments are open it reads "N comments remain open or awaiting backcheck".
- "Screening passes Submission Readiness". If it does not, the panel reads "The screening no longer passes Submission Readiness — the facilitator must
  re-verify the affected pages". Until the readiness check loads, it reads "Submission Readiness not yet confirmed".

To approve, the review lead selects **Approve**, reads the attestation in the **Approve Review** dialog, "I affirm that I have reviewed all submitted
comments and their responses, and that this review has met its criteria.", and selects **Approve Review**. For a level other than the last, the next
level's review period starts at once and its reviewers are notified. The approved level shows "Approved on [date] by [name]", and its comments can no
longer be changed.

:::caution

The approval of a level cannot be retracted. Approving the final level permanently marks the screening as Complete, and its inputs and results stay
locked.

:::

### Rejecting a Review

The review lead rejects the review with **Reject**, in the level's header during the review and resolution periods or in the decision panel during
backcheck. The **Reject Review** dialog requires a **Justification (required)**; **Reject Review** confirms.

:::caution

Rejecting resets all review progress. The screening returns to In Progress, its inputs unlock, and it must pass through every review level again after
it is resubmitted. The comments of the rejected cycle are kept as a read-only record. A rejection cannot be undone.

:::
