# Screening Management

The Screening Management page administers the screening team, the access requests and the review levels with their reviewers. The user
reaches it from **Screening Information > Screening Management**. Only the Facilitator and administrators see the page; for anyone else it does not
appear in the sub-navigation, and following a link to it opens the Overview page.

Changes on this page take effect immediately, with no confirmation step, and DST confirms each one with a notice. The page has nothing to verify. The
screening cannot be submitted until it has at least two review levels and each level has a review lead (see
[Review configuration](#review-configuration)).

## Team management

Team membership is controlled from the **Team Management** card, where people are added, assigned roles and removed. The card manages the same team as the Manage Team dialog
on the Dashboard (see [Managing the screening team](./030-getting-started.mdx#managing-the-screening-team)). Reviewers are not listed here; they are
assigned to each review level under [Review configuration](#review-configuration).

- **Add Member** — opens a search, "Search by name or email...", with a role drop-down. The user chooses the role and selects **+ Add as Team
  Member** or **+ Add as Viewer** on the person. "No users found" appears when nothing matches. **Cancel** closes the search.
- **Team Members** — each person's name and email address, a role drop-down (**Team Member** or **Viewer**) and a remove button. Changing the role or
  removing the person takes effect at once.
- **Facilitator** — the Facilitator's name and email address, or "No facilitator assigned".

### Replacing the Facilitator

Until the screening is submitted, an administrator can replace the Facilitator. The administrator selects **Replace**, searches with "Search for new
facilitator...", and selects **Make Facilitator** on the new user. The panel warns that the current Facilitator "will be removed as Facilitator", and
the previous Facilitator loses access to the screening. For an Official screening of a USACE dam, the search lists only members of the USACE
Facilitators group; for any other screening it searches all users. From submission on, Replace is hidden.

### Changes after submission

Once the screening is submitted, a note at the top of the card says which changes remain possible:

- Under review: "This screening is under review. Team members and Viewers can still be added or removed; the name, description and Facilitator stay
  fixed."
- Complete: "This screening is complete. Only Viewer access can be changed."
- No Facilitator: "This screening needs a facilitator. Only Viewer access can be changed until one is assigned."

While only Viewer access can be changed, roles cannot be changed and only Viewers can be added or removed.

### Access requests

Users ask for access to a screening from the Dashboard's Search Catalog (see [Search Catalog](./030-getting-started.mdx#search-catalog)). The
Facilitator receives a notification and an email when a request arrives, and an **Access Requests** list appears at the top of the Team Management
card, with each requester's name, email address and reason, when one was given.

- The check-mark button, "Approve — grant Viewer access", adds the requester to the team as a Viewer.
- The × button, "Deny request", declines the request.

Either way the request leaves the list, and the requester receives a notification and an email.

## Review configuration

After submission, the screening passes through a series of review levels, in order from top to bottom. The **Review Configuration** card defines
those levels and assigns each level's reviewers and review lead. Every screening starts with **Level 1** and **Level 2**.
[Review Levels](./700-review.mdx#review-levels) describes the review itself.

Each level's header shows its name, a status badge (**Not Started** until the level begins, then the level's review phase), the number of reviewers
and any configuration issue. Selecting the header shows the reviewers.

### Adding and arranging levels

A level can be changed in these ways while it is Not Started:

- **Rename** — the pencil beside the name. The user edits the name and presses Enter or leaves the field; Escape restores the name.
- **Reorder** — dragging the handle on the header moves the level up or down the list. The keyboard can also move a level.
- **Delete** — the trash button removes the level, with the notice "Review level deleted."
- **Add Review Level** — opens a **Level Name** field, for example "District Review". **Create Level** adds the level at the end of the list, and
  **Cancel** closes the field. A screening with no levels shows **Add your first review level** instead.

### Assigning reviewers and the review lead

The reviewers of a level are listed with their names and email addresses, the review lead first with a **Lead** badge:

- **Add Reviewer** — searches users by name or email ("Search by name or email..."), leaving out people already on the level. Selecting a person adds
  that person as a reviewer.
- **Make lead** — the crown button on a reviewer makes that reviewer the level's review lead.
- **Remove reviewer** — the trash button removes the reviewer.

Each level needs a review lead before the screening can be submitted; the lead can add the rest of the review team once the screening is submitted
(see [The review team](./700-review.mdx#the-review-team)). Reviewers can be added and removed only while a level is Not Started or in its review
period, and the review lead can be changed until the level is approved. When the same person is a reviewer on more than one level, a note titled
"Reviewer overlap detected" names the person and the levels.

### Setting the target review cycle

**Target Review Cycle** sets the quarterly cycle in which the National Cadre and Senior Oversight Group review the screening. The drop-down
appears at the top of the Review Configuration card only for a USACE screening that is not a Testing screening and has a **National Cadre Review**
level. The Facilitator or an administrator chooses one of the next 10 fiscal-quarter cycles, named by fiscal year and quarter (for example "FY2027
Q1"), or **— No target cycle —** to clear it. DST confirms with "Target review cycle updated." or "Target review cycle cleared."

Changing the cycle replaces the National Cadre Review level's reviewers with that cycle's participants and the National Cadre chair and vice chair.
If the review lead is not a participant in the new cycle, DST clears the lead, and a new lead must be assigned. The National Cadre and Senior
Oversight Group levels cannot be renamed, deleted or reordered. The cycle can be changed at any stage of the review until the screening is
complete; after that the drop-down is unavailable and reads "This screening is complete — the target review cycle can no longer be changed."
