# Screening Information

The Screening Information module records what a screening is about and who works on it: the screening's description, the dam's data, the
reservoir's operating curves, the screening team, the review levels, and a history of the screening's milestones. A screening opens on this module's
Overview page.

The module has five pages, in sub-navigation order: Overview, Dam Data, Operational Data, Screening Management and Screening Activity. The dam data
and key elevations entered here are the starting point for the Hazards, Response and Consequences modules, and the operating curves feed the
consequence routing and some failure mode evaluations. Dam Data and Operational Data are verified by the screening team (see
[Human Verification](./04-working-in-dst.mdx#human-verification)); the other three pages have nothing to verify.

### Dam location map

The map shows the dam's location from the **Latitude** and **Longitude** on the Dam Data page, marked with a blue pin. It opens on a satellite view
with street labels and zoomed to the dam. The controls at the upper right switch the map style among streets, satellite with streets and outdoors,
and zoom and rotate the map. While the screening stays open, DST remembers the map's position and style, and returns to the dam if its location
changed in the meantime.

A Facilitator or Team Member can set the dam's location on the map while the screening's inputs are open for editing:

has the same effects as changing its coordinates on the Dam Data page (see [Dam data messages](#dam-data-messages)).

### NID values and overrides

For an NID dam, the label of every field filled from the NID carries a small badge:

- **NID** (blue) — the value matches the NID record.
- **Edited** (amber) — the team changed the value. Resting the pointer on the badge shows the NID value and the date DST fetched it.

Selecting an **Edited** badge opens a small panel that shows the **Current** value and the **NID** value
(<FigReference figKey="dst-users-guide-fig-dam-data-reset-to-nid" />). **Reset to NID** returns the field to the NID value, and **Cancel** closes the
panel. Some fields reset together: Latitude and Longitude, the dam types, and the spillway types. When more than one of them changed, the panel lists
each one under "Reset these fields to NID values?".

<Figure
  figKey="dst-users-guide-fig-dam-data-reset-to-nid"

  alt="The Dam Height field on the Dam Data page of Blue Marsh Dam – User's Guide Example, with its amber Edited badge selected. A small panel below the badge is titled 'Reset Dam Height (ft) to NID value?' and lists Current, the value the team entered, and NID, the NID value in blue, above Cancel and Reset to NID buttons."
  caption="Resetting an edited value to the NID value."

Each changed value needs a justification when the NID supplied a value for the field. The justification chip beside the field's label is a pencil
outlined in amber while the justification is missing, and a check mark once it is written. Selecting the chip opens a box headed by the field name,
with the note "The NID value is" followed by the NID value
(<FigReference figKey="dst-users-guide-fig-dam-data-override-justification" />). The user enters the reason, up to 4,000 characters, and the
justification saves when the box closes. The same justifications appear in the [Dam data summary](#dam-data-summary) tray. A field the NID left blank
needs no justification when the team fills it in.

  figKey="dst-users-guide-fig-dam-data-override-justification"

The justification box below the chip is headed Dam Height, reads 'The NID value is' followed by the NID value, and holds the team's justification text."
  caption="The justification for a value that differs from the NID."

A justification is kept when its field is reset to the NID value or re-fetched, and it is shown and required again if the field is changed again. A
missing justification leaves the Dam Data page Incomplete on the Submission Readiness page (see
[Submission Readiness](./04-working-in-dst.mdx#submission-readiness)). Writing or changing a justification clears the page's verification.

:::note

The page does not show the dam's hazard potential classification. For an NID dam, DST copies it from the NID when the screening is created, and a
re-fetch can update it (it appears in the re-fetch comparison as **Hazard Class**). It cannot be entered or changed in DST, so a screening of a dam
entered manually has none.

:::

### Re-fetching from the NID

The NID updates its records over time. When the NID has changed a field this screening uses since the screening's copy was fetched, a note titled
"The NID has newer data for this dam" appears below the NID data bar. It gives the date of the NID update, the number of fields changed and the version
the screening uses.

**Re-fetch from NID** compares the screening's values with the current NID record and opens a comparison below the NID data bar
(<FigReference figKey="dst-users-guide-fig-dam-data-refetch-preview" />). The button is available to the Facilitator and Team Members while the
screening's inputs are open for editing, and only for a dam with a Federal ID.

<Figure
  figKey="dst-users-guide-fig-dam-data-refetch-preview"
  src="figures/web-applications/dst/users-guide/v1.0/figures/dam-data-refetch-preview.png"
  alt="The re-fetch comparison panel below the NID data bar on the Dam Data page of Blue Marsh Dam – User's Guide Example. A summary line reads how many NID updates will apply and how many edits are kept. A Your edits table lists Dam Height with columns Field, Your value and NID, and a Keep mine / Use NID control at the end of the row with Keep mine selected. Apply and Cancel buttons sit at the bottom of the panel."
  caption="Comparing the screening with the current NID record."
  width="80%"
/>

- **NID updates** lists the fields whose NID value changed and that the team has not edited, with the **Current** and **NID** values. These values
  are replaced when the comparison is applied.
- **Your edits** lists the fields the team changed, with **Your value** and the **NID** value. Each row has a **Keep mine** / **Use NID** choice;
  **Keep mine** is selected to start. **Keep all mine** and **Use NID for all** set every row at once when there are two or more. "NID changed since
  your edit" marks a field whose NID value changed after the team edited it, and shows the earlier NID value before the new one.
- The summary line above the tables counts the updates that will apply, the edits kept and the edits that will be replaced. When nothing differs, it
  reads "No values differ from the NID. Applying updates this screening's NID record date."

**Apply** saves the result and updates the NID record date and the fetch date. **Cancel** closes the comparison without changing anything. Applying
a comparison that changes any value clears the page's verification and has the same effects on other pages as editing those values.

### Dam data messages

The page messages button reports each missing required value as a required input (see [Page Messages](./04-working-in-dst.mdx#page-messages)):

- "Provide the Dam Name."
- "Select at least one Dam Type." or, when only a general NID classification is present, "The NID classification "Concrete" is too general for
  analysis. Select a specific Dam Type."
- "Select at least one Spillway Type."
- "Provide the Year Completed.", "Provide the Dam Height.", "Provide the Latitude." and "Provide the Longitude."
- "Provide the Dam Crest Elevation.", "Provide the Spillway Crest Elevation." (with a spillway) and "Provide the Normal Pool Elevation."

When the key elevations sit oddly against each other, DST reports a warning as a notice in the lower right corner each time the page saves. The
warnings never block submission:

- "The Normal Pool Elevation is at or above the Dam Crest Elevation."
- "The Spillway Crest Elevation is at or above the Dam Crest Elevation."
- "The Streambed Elevation is above the Normal Pool Elevation."
- "The Streambed Elevation is at or above the Spillway Crest Elevation."
- "The Normal Pool Elevation is above the crest of an uncontrolled spillway. Check both values."
- "The Dam Crest Elevation is more than the Dam Height above the Streambed Elevation. Check the three values."

The Dam Data page is Ready on the Submission Readiness page when every required value is present, every required justification is written and the page
is verified.

Dam Data feeds other modules, so a change here can change other pages:

- Changing the **Latitude** or **Longitude**, on this page or on the Overview map, retrieves the seismic hazard data for the new location (see
  [Chapter 7, Hazards](./07-hazards.mdx#seismic-hazard)).
- Changing the **Normal Pool** elevation updates the normal pool on the Stage Duration page and can clear that page's verification (see
  [Chapter 7, Hazards](./07-hazards.mdx#stage-duration)).
- Changing the dam types or the **Dam Crest** elevation can change which potential failure modes apply and update Failure Mode Screening (see
  [Chapter 10, Failure Mode Screening and Selection](./10-failure-mode-screening-and-selection.mdx)).
- Changes to Dam Data can clear the verification of failure mode locations whose evaluation uses the changed values (see
  [Chapter 11, Failure Mode Evaluation](./11-failure-mode-evaluation.mdx#verifying-a-location)).

### Team management

The **Team Management** card, with the number of people on the team beside its title, lists the screening's Facilitator and its Team Members and
Viewers. It manages the same team as the Manage Team dialog on the Dashboard (see
[Chapter 3, Getting Started](./03-getting-started.mdx#managing-the-screening-team)). Reviewers are not listed here; they are assigned to each review
level under [Review configuration](#review-configuration).

- **Facilitator** — the Facilitator's name and email address with a **Facilitator** badge, or "No facilitator assigned". An administrator can select
  **Replace**, search for a user with "Search for new facilitator...", and select **Make Facilitator** on that user. The panel warns that the current
  Facilitator "will be removed as Facilitator", and the previous Facilitator loses access to the screening. A user who is already on the team shows
  the current role beside the name.
- **Team Members** — each person's name and email address, a role drop-down (**Team Member** or **Viewer**) and a remove button. Changing the role or
  removing the person takes effect at once. "No team members assigned yet." and **Add your first team member** appear when the list is empty.
- **Add Member** — opens a search, "Search by name or email...", beside a role drop-down. The user chooses the role and selects **+ Add as Team
  Member** or **+ Add as Viewer** on the person. "No users found" appears when nothing matches. **Cancel** closes the search.

Once the screening is submitted, a note at the top of the card explains which changes remain possible:

- Under review: "This screening is under review. Team members and Viewers can still be added or removed; the name, description and Facilitator stay
  fixed."
- Complete: "This screening is complete. Only Viewer access can be changed."
- No Facilitator: "This screening needs a facilitator. Only Viewer access can be changed until one is assigned."

While only Viewer access can be changed, the role drop-downs become labels and only Viewers can be added or removed.

#### Access requests

When users have asked for access to the screening from the Dashboard's Search Catalog (see
[Chapter 3, Getting Started](./03-getting-started.mdx#search-catalog)), an amber **Access Requests** list appears at the top of the Team Management
card, with the number of requests. Each request shows the requester's name and email address and, when one was given, the reason in quotation marks.

- The check-mark button, "Approve — grant Viewer access", adds the requester to the team as a Viewer.
- The × button, "Deny request", declines the request.

Either way the request leaves the list, and the requester receives a notification and an email. The Facilitator receives a notification and an email
when a request arrives.

### Review configuration

The **Review Configuration** card sets up the review levels the screening passes through after it is submitted, in order from top to bottom. Every
screening starts with **Level 1** and **Level 2**. The review itself is described in [Chapter 34, Review](./34-review.mdx#review-levels).

Each level is a card. Its header shows the level name, a status badge (**Not Started** until the level begins, then the level's review phase), the
number of reviewers, and any configuration issue. Selecting the header expands the card to show the reviewers. A level can be changed in these ways
while it is Not Started:

- **Rename** — the pencil beside the name. The user edits the name and presses Enter or leaves the field; Escape restores the name.
- **Reorder** — dragging the handle at the left of the header moves the level up or down the list. The keyboard can also move a level.
- **Delete** — the trash button removes the level, with the notice "Review level deleted." A screening needs at least two levels, so with only two
  the button is unavailable ("A screening needs at least two review levels").
- **Add Review Level** — below the list, opens a **Level Name** field, for example "District Review". **Create Level** adds the level at the end of
  the list, and **Cancel** closes the field.

The reviewers of a level are listed with their names and email addresses, the review lead first with a **Lead** badge:

- **Add Reviewer** — searches users by name or email ("Search by name or email..."). Selecting a person adds that person as a reviewer.
- **Make lead** — the crown button on a reviewer makes that reviewer the level's review lead.
- **Remove reviewer** — the trash button removes the reviewer. After submission, the review lead and reviewers with comments in the current review
  cycle cannot be removed; the button explains why.

A level without a review lead shows **No review lead** in its header, explained as "A review lead must be assigned before the screening can be
submitted. The lead can add the rest of the review team once the screening is submitted." Reviewers can be added and removed only while a level is
Not Started or in its review period, and the review lead can be changed until the level is approved. After submission, a level's review lead can also
add and remove its reviewers (see [Chapter 34, Review](./34-review.mdx#a-review-level)). When the same person is a reviewer on more than one level, a
note titled "Reviewer overlap detected" names the person and the levels.

The Submission Readiness page reports the configuration that is still missing, for example "Set up at least two review levels in Screening
Management." or "Assign a review lead to Level 1."

Screenings of USACE dams in the national review workflow also have National Cadre and Senior Oversight Group levels. These levels show a lock instead of
a drag handle, and DST staffs them from the review cycle chosen in **Target Review Cycle** at the top of the card; their reviewers cannot be changed
here. These levels are described in [Chapter 34, Review](./34-review.mdx#national-review-levels).
