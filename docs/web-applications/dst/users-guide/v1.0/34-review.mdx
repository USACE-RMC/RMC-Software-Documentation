# Review

After the Facilitator submits a screening for review, it passes through its review levels one at a time. At each level, the assigned reviewers
comment on the screening's pages, the screening team responds to each comment, the reviewers check the responses, and the level's review lead
approves the level. When the last level is approved, the screening is Complete.

This chapter is written for reviewers and review leads as well as for the screening team. It describes the roles in review, the phases each level
passes through, the Review Levels page, the page of a single level, and the steps for commenting, responding, backchecking and deciding. The
Submission Readiness page and the act of submitting are described in [Chapter 4, Working in DST](./04-working-in-dst.mdx#submission-readiness).
The review levels and their reviewers are set up in the Review Configuration section of the Screening Management page (see
[Chapter 5, Screening Information](./05-screening-information.mdx#screening-management)).

Everyone assigned to a screening can open the Review module and read every level's comments. Reviewers also read every page of the screening; the
inputs are read-only to them. Selecting **Review** in the module navigation opens a different page for each role:

- A reviewer on a level that is in progress opens that level's page directly.
- Before submission, the Facilitator, Team Members and administrators open the **Submission Readiness** page.
- Everyone else opens the **Review Levels** page.

## The Review Lifecycle

A review level passes through five phases. Its phase appears as a badge on the Review Levels page and on the Dashboard's My Reviews tab (see
[Chapter 3, Getting Started](./03-getting-started.mdx#my-reviews)). <TableReference tableKey="dst-users-guide-table-review-phases" /> lists the
phases in order.

The first level starts its review period as soon as the screening is submitted (see
[Submitting for review](./04-working-in-dst.mdx#submitting-for-review)). Each later level starts its review period as soon as the level before it is
approved, and its reviewers are notified. While inputs are locked, a strip across the top of every page explains why (see
[Input Lock](./04-working-in-dst.mdx#input-lock)).

The resolution and backcheck phases repeat in rounds until every comment is closed. Each round begins when a returned comment sends the level back to
Resolution, and the level's page shows the round number.

A review lead can reject the level at any time from its review period through its backcheck. A rejection ends the review of the whole screening, not
only of that level (see [Approving or Rejecting a Level](#approving-or-rejecting-a-level)).

### The review team

**Review Team**, shown to the Facilitator, administrators and the level's review lead, opens a dialog listing the level's reviewers, review lead first and marked
**(Lead)**. During and after the review period, each reviewer has a participation status:

- **Pending**: the reviewer has not yet commented or submitted No Comments.
- **Participated**: the reviewer has commented or submitted No Comments. The review lead is counted as Participated when the review period ends.
- **Unresponsive**: the reviewer did not participate.

Once the review period starts, the button shows the participation count, for example **Review Team (1/2)** for one participant of two
reviewers; on a Pending level it shows the number of reviewers, such as **Review Team (2)**. The dialog's controls depend on the phase:

- **Adding and removing reviewers.** While the level is Pending or in its review period, a search box adds a reviewer, and the trash icon beside a
  reviewer removes the reviewer. Reviewers added during the review period are notified and can comment at once. The review lead has no trash icon.
  A reviewer who has commented in the current cycle cannot be removed; that reviewer's trash icon is unavailable, with the reason "This reviewer
  has comments in this review cycle and can't be removed." During resolution
  and backcheck the team cannot be changed.
- **Marking Unresponsive.** During the active phases the review lead can select **Mark Unresponsive** beside a Pending reviewer, enter a
  **Justification (required)** and select **Confirm**. **Remove Unresponsive** reverses it, except during backcheck. When the review lead ends the
  review period, every reviewer still Pending is marked Unresponsive.

Only the Facilitator or an administrator assigns the review lead, on the Screening Management page.

While the Comment box is empty, Category and Sub-category follow the page on screen as the reviewer moves around the screening. Once the box holds
text they stay fixed, and the draft is kept when the panel is closed and opened again. **Clear** empties the form. **Submit Comment** adds the

Selecting a comment opens it in the comment panel over the current page.

### No Comments

Reviewers add comments with **Add a review comment** only during the review period. During the resolution period a reviewer can still import
comments or submit No Comments from the level's page (see [Importing and exporting comments](#importing-and-exporting-comments)).

A reviewer with nothing to raise selects **No Comments** on the level's page. The "Submit 'No Comments'" dialog explains that the reviewer is attesting
to having no comments for the level and will not be able to add regular comments without reverting the entry; **Submit No Comments** confirms. The
entry appears in the table with the status Concurrence and counts as participation. **No Comments** is not offered to a reviewer who already has
comments on the level. During the review period, **Revert 'No Comments'** removes the entry.

### Editing and deleting a comment

During the review period, the author of a comment sees **Edit** and **Delete** in its panel. **Edit** opens the comment's Category, Sub-category,
Comment and **Mark as critical** for change; **Save changes** keeps the change and **Cancel** discards it. **Delete** asks for confirmation:

:::caution

Deleting a comment permanently removes it from the review record ("It cannot be undone."). Only the author can delete a comment, and only during the
review period.

:::

### Ending the review period

The review lead ends the review period with **End Review Period**, on the Review Levels page or on the level's page. The confirmation dialog states
that reviewers can no longer submit or revise comments, that the screening team can begin responding, and which reviewers will be marked
Unresponsive because they have not participated. Selecting **End Review Period** in the dialog moves the level to its resolution period, unlocks the
screening's inputs and notifies the screening team.

### Submitting for backcheck

When every comment has a response, the Facilitator selects **Submit for Backcheck** in the strip. The button stays unavailable, with the reason when
the pointer rests on it, while "Every open comment needs a team response before backcheck." or while "The screening must pass Submission Readiness
before backcheck. Re-verify any page your changes affected." The confirmation dialog states that screening inputs lock until the reviewers finish,
that the level's reviewers are notified to close or return each of their comments, and that inputs unlock for another resolution round if any comment
is returned. Selecting **Submit for Backcheck** in the dialog starts the backcheck period.

## Backcheck

During the backcheck period, each reviewer checks the team's responses to the reviewer's own comments. The strip above the level reads, for example,
"Backcheck — round 1" and "2 comments awaiting backcheck". The author of a Responded comment sees a reply form below the thread
(<FigReference figKey="dst-users-guide-fig-review-comment-backcheck" />).

  figKey="dst-users-guide-fig-review-comment-backcheck"

The reviewer chooses one of three actions:

- **Close without response** closes the comment. The thread ends with a **Closed without response** entry.
- **Close with response** records the text in the reply box as a Backcheck reply and closes the comment.
- **Return to team** records the reply and returns the comment to the team with the status Open.

The two actions that record a reply stay unavailable until the box holds text. New comments cannot be added during backcheck; to raise a further
request, the reviewer returns the original comment with a reply. When no comment is left awaiting backcheck, DST ends the round:

- If any comment was returned, the level goes back to its resolution period for the next round, the inputs unlock and the screening team is
  notified.
- If every comment is closed, the strip reads "All comments closed — awaiting the review lead's approval" and the review lead is notified.

A closed comment can be reopened with **Reopen** by its author or the review lead, but only during the phase in which it was closed. Reopening returns the comment to Responded when the team has responded, and to Open
otherwise.

### Review lead actions

On another reviewer's comment, the review lead sees a **Review Lead Actions** box:

- **Force Close**, while the comment is Open or Responded, closes it. The lead enters a **Force close justification (required for the record)** and
  selects **Confirm Force Close**. The justification appears in the comment's Closed entry.
- **Backcheck on Behalf**, during backcheck, replies for an author who cannot. The lead enters a **Backcheck comment** and a **Lead justification
  (required for the record)** and selects **Submit Backcheck**, which returns the comment to the team.

When the review lead opens a level in its backcheck period, an **Official Review Decision** panel appears above the level's heading
(<FigReference figKey="dst-users-guide-fig-review-decision-panel" />). It summarizes the reviewers accounted for and the comments closed, and lists the
**Approval Criteria**, each marked met or not met.

**Approve** is available only when all three criteria are met:

- "All reviewers accounted for (N participated or marked unresponsive)". While a reviewer is still Pending it reads "N of M reviewers have not
  participated".
- "All N comments closed", or "No comments were submitted". While comments are open it reads "N comments remain open or awaiting backcheck".
- "Screening passes Submission Readiness". If it does not, the panel reads "The screening no longer passes Submission Readiness — the facilitator must
  re-verify the affected pages". Until the readiness check loads, it reads "Submission Readiness not yet confirmed".

Selecting **Approve** opens the **Approve Review** dialog with an attestation: "I affirm that I have reviewed all submitted comments and their
responses, and that this review has met its criteria." **Approve Review** confirms. For a level other than the last, the next level's review period
starts at once and its reviewers are notified.

:::caution

The approval of a level cannot be retracted. Approving the final level permanently marks the screening as Complete, and its inputs and results stay
locked. The one exception is a Senior Oversight Group decision (see [National Review Levels](#national-review-levels)).

:::

After approval, the level shows "Approved on [date] by [name]", and its comments can no longer be changed.

### Rejecting a review

The review lead can reject the review with **Reject**, in the level's header during the review and resolution periods or in the decision panel during
backcheck. The **Reject Review** dialog requires a **Justification (required)** and explains the result; **Reject Review** confirms.

:::caution

Rejecting resets all review progress. The screening returns to In Progress, its inputs unlock, and it must pass through every review level again after
it is resubmitted. The comments of the rejected cycle are kept as a read-only record. A rejection cannot be undone.

:::

## National Review Levels

Some screenings follow a national review workflow with three levels: District Quality Control Review, National Cadre Review and Senior Oversight
Group Review. New screenings start with Level 1 and Level 2 instead. The District Quality Control Review works as described in this chapter. The two
national levels differ:

- **National Cadre Review.** Reviewers are assigned automatically from the National Cadre (NC) for the review cycle the level targets, shown on
  its card. Reviewers comment as on other levels. NC leadership submits a tentative decision with **Submit Tentative Decision**, which ends the
  review period. The decision records a **DSAC Rating** (Dam Safety Action Classification), **Tolerability**, **DSAC Justification** and **Path
  Forward**. NC members vote in a voting session, and the NC Chair or Vice Chair confirms or rejects the decision. A National Cadre level has no
  separate backcheck period and no End Review Period button.
- **Senior Oversight Group Review.** The Senior Oversight Group (SOG) level appears as its own card on the Review Levels page and takes no comments.
  After every earlier level is approved, the SOG Chair or Vice Chair accepts or rejects the screening with a decision of the same fields, or approves
  a recommendation entered by the SOG Executive Recorder. When the National Cadre voted unanimously for DSAC 4 or 5, the SOG instead acknowledges
  the review with **Acknowledge SOG Review**.

Accepting or acknowledging the SOG review marks the screening Complete. The SOG Chair or Vice Chair can retract an accepted decision with
**Retract Decision**, a **Retraction justification (required)** and **Confirm Retraction**; the SOG level returns to Pending and the screening to
Under Review.
