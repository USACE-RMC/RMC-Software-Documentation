# Results Map

The Results Map page, at **Consequences > Results Map**, shows the consequences of one loading level on the map. It is view-only; its choices are
shared with the Results Data page and reset when the page is reloaded.

<FigReference figKey="dst-users-guide-fig-results-map-page" /> shows the page with a structure selected, and
<TableReference tableKey="dst-users-guide-table-consequences-results-map-regions" /> describes each numbered region.

- **Consequence Results**, the panel at the upper left, has a card for each consequence function, marked Failure or Non-fail, and a **Loading level**
  picker showing each level's stage and life loss or status. The picked level's maximum depth grid is drawn on the map.
- **Colour structures by** colors the structures by **Mean life loss** (the default), **Arrival after the breach**, **Population at risk**,
  **Depth × velocity**, **Maximum depth**, **Mean damage** or **Collapse share**, in the classes the legend shows. **Wet structures only** (on by
  default) hides the structures that stay dry.
- Selecting a structure opens its panel: its occupancy and construction, its life loss, population, fatality rate, collapse share, depth, depth ×
  velocity, arrival time and damage at the level, and a table of its results across the function's levels. Selecting the dam opens the dam panel,
  with the outflow plot, peak outflow and hazard occurrence.
- **Structures**, a tray at the bottom, lists the 20 wet structures ranked by the **Order by** measure. Selecting a row moves the map to it, and
  **All structures (.csv)** downloads every wet structure.
