# Results Map

The Results Map page, at **Consequences > Results Map**, shows the consequences of one loading level on the map. It is view-only; its choices are
shared with the Results Data page and reset when the page is reloaded.

- **Consequence Results**, the panel at the upper left, has a card for each consequence function, marked Failure or Non-fail, and a **Loading level**
  picker showing each level's stage and life loss or status. The picked level's maximum depth grid is drawn on the map.
- **Colour structures by** colors the structures by **Mean life loss** (the default), **Arrival after the breach**, **Population at risk**,
  **Depth × velocity**, **Maximum depth**, **Mean damage** or **Collapse share**, in the classes the legend shows. **Wet structures only** (on by
  default) hides the structures that stay dry.
- Selecting a structure opens its panel: its occupancy and construction, its life loss, population, fatality rate, collapse share, depth, depth ×
  velocity, arrival time and damage at the level, and a table of its results across the function's levels. Selecting the dam opens the dam panel,
  with the outflow plot, peak outflow and hazard occurrence.
- **Structures**, a tray at the bottom, lists the 20 wet structures ranked by the **Order by** measure. Selecting a row moves the map to it, and
  **All structures (.csv)** downloads every wet structure.

## Structure classes

Each **Colour structures by** measure has fixed classes, so the same color means the same thing at every loading level and in every screening. The
legend lists them worst first, with the number of structures in each, and on the map the structures of the worse classes are drawn on top of the
others. The classes are:

- **Mean life loss**: 1 or more lives, 0.1 to 1, 0.01 to 0.1, and Under 0.01.
- **Arrival after the breach**: Before the breach, Within 30 min, 30 min to 1 h, 1 to 2 h, 2 to 4 h, and After 4 h.
- **Population at risk**: 10 or more people, 5 to 10, 2 to 5, and Under 2.
- **Depth × velocity**: 50 ft²/s or more, 20 to 50, 7 to 20, and Under 7.
- **Maximum depth**: 15 ft or more, 6 to 15 ft, 2 to 6 ft, and Under 2 ft, drawn in the blues of the depth grid.
- **Mean damage**: $1M or more, $250k to $1M, $50k to $250k, and Under $50k.
- **Collapse share**: In half the iterations or more, 10% to 50%, Under 10%, and Never, which is drawn in neutral gray.

Each class takes its lower bound and runs up to the next class's, so a mean life loss of exactly 1 is in "1 or more lives". Below the classes, the
legend adds a row for the structures with nothing to class: "No arrival time" for **Arrival after the breach**, and "No value" for the other
measures. When **Wet structures only** is off, a last row, "Stayed dry", counts the structures the water did not reach, which are drawn in gray.
If the structure inventory has changed since the level ran, the dry structures cannot be drawn, and the legend says so.

## Arrival times

Arrival times count from the breach start of the loading level. At a level without a breach start, they count from when the first structure got
wet, and the legend notes "Times after the first structure wet: this level has no breach start."

An arrival time can be negative. A structure that the water reached before the breach started, for example from the inflow or from overtopping
before the breach, falls in the **Before the breach** class. The structure panel gives its time as, for example, "51 min before the breach", and the
tray's **Water arrives** column as a negative time such as "−51 min". Times counted from the first structure wet are never negative.

## Structures tray

The tray at the bottom of the map is headed **Structures** with the function and loading level, for example "Structures · Overtopping, 817 ft".
Opening it slides it up over the map. Its **Order by** picks the measure the list is ranked by, and changes **Colour structures by** to match; the
line beside it says what the list holds, for example "The 20 of 34 wet structures with the highest mean life loss, as the map colours them." The
list's columns are **Structure**, **Occupancy**, **Mean life loss**, **Fatality rate**, **Population**, **Maximum depth**, **Depth × velocity**,
**Water arrives**, **Collapsed** and **Mean damage**. Selecting a row closes the tray and shows the structure on the map.

When the level's structures cannot be listed, the tray says why, for example:

- "Entered as tables, so it has no structures to list.", for a function entered by hand on the Results Data page.
- "This analysis has no loading levels yet."
- "This loading level has no result yet, so it has no structures to list."
- "Reading this level's structures…", while they load.
- "No structure got wet at this level."
