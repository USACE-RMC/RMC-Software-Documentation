# Results Map

The Results Map page (**Consequences > Results Map**) shows where the consequences of one loading level fall and which structures drive them. The
page is view-only. Its choices are shared with the Results Data page and reset when the page is reloaded.

## Viewing a loading level

To view the consequences of a loading level, the user selects a function card, then a level in the **Loading level** selector. The map draws that
level's maximum depth grid and colors the structures.

- **Colour structures by** colors the structures by **Mean life loss** (the default), **Arrival after the breach**, **Population at risk**,
  **Depth × velocity**, **Maximum depth**, **Mean damage** or **Collapse share**. **Wet structures only** (on by default) hides the structures that
  stay dry.
- Selecting a structure opens its panel: its occupancy and construction; its life loss, population, fatality rate, collapse share, depth, depth ×
  velocity, arrival time and damage at the level; and a table of its results across the function's levels.
- Selecting the dam opens the dam panel, with the outflow plot, peak outflow and hazard occurrence.

## Structure classes

Each **Colour structures by** measure has fixed classes, so a color means the same at every loading level. The legend lists them worst first, with
the number of structures in each:

- **Mean life loss**: 1 or more lives, 0.1 to 1, 0.01 to 0.1, and Under 0.01.
- **Arrival after the breach**: Before the breach, Within 30 min, 30 min to 1 h, 1 to 2 h, 2 to 4 h, and After 4 h.
- **Population at risk**: 10 or more people, 5 to 10, 2 to 5, and Under 2.
- **Depth × velocity**: 50 ft²/s or more, 20 to 50, 7 to 20, and Under 7.
- **Maximum depth**: 15 ft or more, 6 to 15 ft, 2 to 6 ft, and Under 2 ft.
- **Mean damage**: \$1M or more, \$250k to \$1M, \$50k to \$250k, and Under \$50k.
- **Collapse share**: In half the iterations or more, 10% to 50%, Under 10%, and Never.

Each class runs from its lower bound up to the next class's, so a mean life loss of exactly 1 is in "1 or more lives". The legend adds a row for
structures with nothing to class ("No arrival time" or "No value"), and, when **Wet structures only** is off, a "Stayed dry" row.

## Arrival times

Arrival times count from the breach start of the loading level. At a level without a breach start, they count from when the first structure became
wet, and the legend notes "Times after the first structure wet: this level has no breach start."

A structure the water reached before the breach started, for example from the inflow or from overtopping, falls in the **Before the breach** class.
The structure panel gives its time as, for example, "51 min before the breach", and the tray as a negative time such as "−51 min".

## Structures tray

The **Structures** tray identifies the structures with the worst consequences. Headed with the function and loading level, for example
"Structures · Overtopping, 817 ft", it lists the 20 wet structures ranked by **Order by**, which also changes **Colour structures by** to match. The
line beside it says what the list holds, for example "The 20 of 34 wet structures with the highest mean life loss, as the map colours them." The
columns are **Structure**, **Occupancy**, **Mean life loss**, **Fatality rate**, **Population**, **Maximum depth**, **Depth × velocity**, **Water
arrives**, **Collapsed** and **Mean damage**.

Selecting a row closes the tray and shows the structure on the map. **All structures (.csv)** downloads every wet structure.
