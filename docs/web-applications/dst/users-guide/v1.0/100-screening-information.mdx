# Screening Information

The Screening Information module records what a screening is about and who works on it: the screening's description, the dam's data, the
reservoir's operating curves, the screening team, the review levels, and a history of the screening's milestones. A screening opens on this module's
Overview page.

The module has five pages, in sub-navigation order: [Overview](./101-overview.mdx), [Dam Data](./102-dam-data.mdx),
[Operational Data](./103-operational-data.mdx), [Screening Management](./104-screening-management.mdx) and
[Screening Activity](./105-screening-activity.mdx). The dam data
and key elevations entered here are the starting point for the Hazards, Response and Consequences modules, and the operating curves feed the
consequence routing and some failure mode evaluations. The screening team verifies Dam Data and Operational Data (see
[Human Verification](./040-working-in-dst.mdx#human-verification)); the other three pages have nothing to verify.
