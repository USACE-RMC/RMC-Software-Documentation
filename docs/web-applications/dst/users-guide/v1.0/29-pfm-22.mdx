# PFM-22: Gate Failure

Potential failure mode 22 (PFM-22), Gate Failure, is failure of a spillway gate, where the loading on the gate overstresses its strut arms, girders or diaphragms and the load cannot be redistributed to other members. The loading is the reservoir pool on the gate, evaluated at stages from the spillway crest through the top of gates to the top of dam.

[Chapter 11, Failure Mode Evaluation](./11-failure-mode-evaluation.mdx#event-trees) describes the parts of the page that every failure mode page shares.

The user opens the page by selecting a PFM-22 location on the Failure Mode Evaluation page (see [Opening a location](./11-failure-mode-evaluation.mdx#opening-a-location)).

PFM-22 reads the top of gates from one gated concrete cross section. The **Cross Section** drop-down lists only the dam's concrete cross sections that have a gate on the Dam Information page (see [Chapter 8, Dam Information](./08-dam-information.mdx)). The top of the selected section's closed gate becomes the Top
of Gates stage, and choosing a different section moves that stage. When no gated section is selected, or the gate has
no closed top elevation, DST places the Top of Gates stage a third of the way from the spillway crest to the top of dam. The top of dam does not come
from the selected section; it is the lowest crest across the dam's earth embankment and non-overflow concrete cross sections.

When the selected section is deleted on the Dam Information page, DST removes it from the location. The Top of Gates stage then falls back to a third
of the way from the spillway crest to the top of dam, the page shows "Select a cross section for this location." if the dam has another gated concrete
section, and the location's verification clears.

Each location of PFM-22 has its own cross section, stages, node probabilities and results. PFM-22 has no limit on the number of
locations, and a copied location keeps the cross section, stages, node probabilities and rationales of the location it copies
(see [Locations](./11-failure-mode-evaluation.mdx#locations) and [Copying a location](./11-failure-mode-evaluation.mdx#copying-a-location)).

## Stages to Evaluate

three default stages, which DST places from other pages. <TableReference tableKey="dst-users-guide-table-pfm-22-default-stages" /> lists them with the
text of their **Source** column. The user cannot edit a default stage's elevation on this page. When its source is missing, the stage's elevation reads
"Source missing" and the page messages name the input to complete (see [Page Messages](#page-messages)).

## User-Defined SRP

With **User defined** selected in the toolbar, the page replaces Stages to evaluate, Event tree evaluation and System Response with a table of
**Pool Elevation (ft)** and **Probability** pairs and its chart, and the entered curve becomes the location's SRP. DST keeps the stages, node
probabilities and rationales, and shows them again when the user selects **Event tree** (see [User-defined SRP](./11-failure-mode-evaluation.mdx#user-defined-srp)).

## Page Messages

The page messages button lists the location's required inputs, warnings and errors (see [Page Messages](./04-working-in-dst.mdx#page-messages)).

<TableReference tableKey="dst-users-guide-table-pfm-22-messages" /> lists the messages that come from the PFM-22 stage sources and cross section, and
Chapter 11 lists the messages that every event-tree page shows (see [Event tree messages](./11-failure-mode-evaluation.mdx#event-tree-messages)). Text
in square brackets stands for the value shown. "Select a cross section for this location." appears only when the dam has at least one gated concrete
cross section. The message about a cross section that no longer has a gate appears when the gate is removed from the selected section on the Dam
Information page. The dam crest message appears when no earth embankment or non-overflow concrete cross section has a drawn crest and Dam Data has no
dam crest elevation.
