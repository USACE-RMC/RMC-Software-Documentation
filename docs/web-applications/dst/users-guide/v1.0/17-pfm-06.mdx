# PFM-06: CLE due to Poor Compaction Adjacent to a Cliff or Wall

Potential failure mode 06 (PFM-06) is concentrated leak erosion (CLE) through poorly compacted embankment fill placed against a cliff, wall or
other rigid face. The flaw extends from the crest down the face, and the reservoir (headwater) loads it whenever the pool rises above the base of the
flaw. The PFM-06 page characterizes the wall or cliff and the fill against it, reads the seepage pathway from an embankment cross section, and
produces the system response probability (SRP) curve for each location. The parts of the page shared with every potential failure mode page are
described in [Chapter 11, Failure Mode Evaluation](./11-failure-mode-evaluation.mdx#the-failure-mode-page).

The user opens a PFM-06 location from the Failure Mode Evaluation page (**Response > Failure Mode Evaluation**, then the location under PFM-06; see
[Opening a location](./11-failure-mode-evaluation.mdx#opening-a-location)). The page heading reads **06 - CLE due to Poor Compaction Adjacent to a
Cliff or Wall**. <FigReference figKey="dst-users-guide-fig-pfm-06-page" /> shows the page, and
<TableReference tableKey="dst-users-guide-table-pfm-06-regions" /> describes each numbered region.

## Location and Cross Section

PFM-06 locations follow the structure contacts selected in question 5 of the Failure Mode Screening page, "Select the locations where embankment was
placed against a structure, wall, or cliff." DST creates one location for each selected contact, in this order, and names it for the contact:

- "Left Abutment Cliff" for **Left abutment cliff or vertical excavation**.
- "Right Abutment Cliff" for **Right abutment cliff or vertical excavation**.
- "Concrete Structure" for **Concrete dam, spillway, powerhouse, or non-overflow section**.
- "Wall" for **Spillway wall, lock wall, or retaining wall**.
- The text entered for **Other Location**, or "Other Location" when that text is blank.

These locations are renamed, added and removed when the answer to question 5 changes, and their **Location** name is read-only on the page. A left
or right abutment cliff location is hidden while a crest-reaching cutoff wall ties into that abutment, and it returns with its data when the wall no
longer does. When question 5 has no contact selected, the failure mode has a single location named "Location 1", whose name the user may edit. PFM-06
locations cannot be copied. The toolbar itself is described in [The page toolbar](./11-failure-mode-evaluation.mdx#the-page-toolbar), and location
management in [Locations](./11-failure-mode-evaluation.mdx#locations).

## Wall or Cliff Flaw and Seepage Pathway

The **Wall or cliff flaw and seepage pathway** section describes the wall or cliff and the fill against it, and shows the pathway DST reads from the
cross section. The section follows the characterization pattern described in
[Characterization](./11-failure-mode-evaluation.mdx#characterization). On a wide screen the inputs, readings and pathway figure sit side by side; on
a narrower screen the readings move to a band below them.

The inputs set the flaw and the material that governs initiation. <TableReference tableKey="dst-users-guide-table-pfm-06-inputs" /> lists them.
When question 12 of the Failure Mode Screening page reports leakage adjacent to the wall or cliff at this location, the three category drop-downs
are disabled and dimmed. The pointer resting on one of them shows "Not used while leakage adjacent to the wall or cliff is observed: the flaw is
carried as certain." Each drop-down's information button lists the definition of every option.

### Pathway figure

The pathway figure draws the selected cross section to scale with its material zones, a legend of the materials present, and a shaded band for the
headwater analysis range. A horizontal line labeled "Failure pathway" crosses the section at the base of the flaw and ends at the first drain or
filter it meets; the legend shows whether the pathway meets a filter, and marks a cutoff wall or upstream facing when the Dam Information page has
one. Callouts at the side give the elevation of the **Crest**, the **Top of wall** of a cutoff wall below the crest, and the **Base of flaw**.

Until DST resolves the pathway, the caption reads "The pathway is read from the drawing once the model has run." followed by the calculation state;
afterwards it reads "Drawn to scale from the selected cross section." with the elevation units. Without a selected cross section the figure shows a
placeholder. The cross-section figure is described in [Characterization](./11-failure-mode-evaluation.mdx#characterization).

### Node assumption cards

A row of cards under the pathway figure states what DST assumes at each node of the PFM-06 event tree, from the inputs and the current results:

- **Flaw** appears only when leakage is observed at this location, and reads "Leakage adjacent to the wall or cliff has been observed. The presence of
  a continuous flaw was assigned a probability of 1."
- **Initiation** states that initiation is evaluated for pools above the failure pathway, and whether the critical shear stress and the initial
  pipe diameter come from defaults or from entered distributions.
- **Filter compatibility** names the drain or filter the pathway reaches and the materials evaluated against it, or reports that the pathway reaches
  no filter.
- **Roof support** names the pathway materials that can hold up the roof of an eroding pipe.
- **Flow limitation** appears only when an upstream element, such as a cutoff wall, limits flow, and describes it.
- **Crack filling** appears only when shell material may fill the flaw.

### Advanced initiation overrides

The **Advanced initiation overrides** bar opens a tray with its description "Screening defaults come from the governing initiation material and stay
locked until a site-specific distribution is chosen." The bar counts the overridden parameters and those that still need a justification.
<TableReference tableKey="dst-users-guide-table-pfm-06-overrides" /> lists the two parameters. Each card's drop-down starts at **Default** with the
default distribution; choosing **Deterministic**, **Uniform**, **Triangular** or **PERT** unlocks its parameter boxes. **Revert to defaults** returns
both parameters to their defaults. Override trays, distribution cards and their justifications are described in
[Parameter overrides](./11-failure-mode-evaluation.mdx#parameter-overrides).

## System Response

The **System Response** section shows the PFM-06 SRP curve, its table and the eight nodal tables, as described in
[System Response](./11-failure-mode-evaluation.mdx#system-response), [SRP charts](./11-failure-mode-evaluation.mdx#srp-charts) and
[Nodal tables](./11-failure-mode-evaluation.mdx#nodal-tables). The chart plots **System Response Probability** on a logarithmic axis from 1E-6
against **Headwater Elevation (ft)**, and **Download SRP curves as a CSV file** exports the curves.
<TableReference tableKey="dst-users-guide-table-pfm-06-nodes" /> lists the nodes. A pool-independent node takes one value for every headwater.
Revising a node is described in [Nodal revisions](./11-failure-mode-evaluation.mdx#nodal-revisions), and a notice about out-of-date results in
[Calculation currency](./11-failure-mode-evaluation.mdx#calculation-currency).

## User-Defined SRP

With **User Defined SRP** selected, the characterization and system response sections are replaced by a grid of headwater elevations and
probabilities and its chart, with **Headwater Elevation (ft)** on the x axis. The grid and the switch between modes are described in
[User-defined SRP](./11-failure-mode-evaluation.mdx#user-defined-srp) and [Active SRP](./11-failure-mode-evaluation.mdx#active-srp).
