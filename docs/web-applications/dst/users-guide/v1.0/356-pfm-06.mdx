# PFM-06: CLE due to Poor Compaction Adjacent to a Cliff or Wall

In potential failure mode 06 (PFM-06), concentrated leak erosion (CLE) develops through poorly compacted fill placed against a cliff, wall or other
rigid face, along a flaw that runs from the crest down the face. The reservoir loads the flaw whenever the pool rises above its base. The PFM-06 page
estimates the system response probability (SRP) for this mechanism. [Failure Mode Pages](./342-failure-mode-pages.mdx) and [System
Response](./343-system-response.mdx) describe the parts every failure mode page shares.

The user opens a PFM-06 location from **Response > Failure Mode Evaluation** (see [Opening a Location](./341-locations.mdx#opening-a-location)).
The page heading reads **06 - CLE due to Poor Compaction Adjacent to a Cliff or Wall**. To evaluate a location:

## Location and Cross Section

PFM-06 locations follow the structure contacts selected in question 5 of the Failure Mode Screening page, "Select the locations where embankment was
placed against a structure, wall, or cliff." DST creates one location for each selected contact, in this order, and names it for the contact:

- "Left Abutment Cliff" for **Left abutment cliff or vertical excavation**.
- "Right Abutment Cliff" for **Right abutment cliff or vertical excavation**.
- "Concrete Structure" for **Concrete dam, spillway, powerhouse, or non-overflow section**.
- "Wall" for **Spillway wall, lock wall, or retaining wall**.
- The text entered for **Other Location**, or "Other Location" when that text is blank.

To add, remove or rename a PFM-06 location, the user changes the answer to question 5; the **Location** name is read-only on the page. DST adds a
location from question 5 only while PFM-06 is selected for evaluation. A left or right abutment cliff location is hidden while a crest-reaching
cutoff wall ties into that abutment, and it returns with its data when the wall no longer does. When question 5 has no contact selected, the failure
mode has a single location named "Location 1", whose name the user may edit.
[The Page Toolbar](./342-failure-mode-pages.mdx#the-page-toolbar) describes the toolbar, and
<TableReference tableKey="dst-users-guide-table-pfm-06-toolbar-inputs" /> lists its inputs.

:::note[Availability]
PFM-06 locations cannot be copied or added on the Failure Mode Evaluation page.
:::

:::caution

Clearing a contact in question 5 deletes its PFM-06 location with the location's inputs, results and justifications. Selecting the first contact in
question 5 deletes the manual "Location 1" in the same way.

:::

## Wall or Cliff Flaw and Seepage Pathway

The **Wall or cliff flaw and seepage pathway** section describes the wall or cliff and the fill against it, and presents for review the pathway DST
reads from the cross section. The section follows the characterization pattern (see [Characterization](./342-failure-mode-pages.mdx#characterization)).

### Pathway Figure

The pathway figure draws the selected cross section to scale with the "Failure pathway" line at the base of the flaw. The pathway ends at the first
drain or filter it meets, and the legend shows whether it meets one. Labels give the elevations of the **Crest**, the **Base of flaw** and, for a
cutoff wall below the crest, the **Top of wall**. Until DST resolves the pathway, the caption reads "The pathway is read from the drawing once the
model has run." [Characterization](./342-failure-mode-pages.mdx#characterization) describes the cross-section figure.

### Node Assumption Cards

The cards state what DST assumes at each node of the PFM-06 system response, from the inputs and the current results:

- **Flaw** appears only when leakage is observed at this location, and reads "Leakage adjacent to the wall or cliff has been observed. The presence of
  a continuous flaw was assigned a probability of 1."
- **Initiation** states that initiation is evaluated for pools above the failure pathway, and whether the critical shear stress and the initial
  pipe diameter come from defaults or from entered distributions.
- **Filter compatibility** names the drain or filter the pathway reaches and the materials evaluated against it, or reports that the pathway reaches
  no filter.
- **Roof support** names the pathway materials that can hold up the roof of an eroding pipe.
- **Flow limitation** appears only when an upstream element, such as a cutoff wall, limits flow, and describes it.
- **Crack filling** appears only when shell material may fill the flaw.

<TableReference tableKey="dst-users-guide-table-pfm-06-nodes" /> lists the nodes. A pool-independent node takes one value for every headwater. [Nodal

## User-Defined SRP

With **User Defined SRP** selected, the page replaces the characterization and system response sections with a grid of headwater elevations and
probabilities and its chart. [User-Defined SRP](./343-system-response.mdx#user-defined-srp) and [Active SRP](./343-system-response.mdx#active-srp)
describe the grid and the switch between modes.
