reportAbstract: Technical reference documenting the concentrated leak erosion (CLE) wall or cliff compaction screening model in the System Response library. The model computes a system response probability (SRP) at each headwater level for concentrated leak erosion through a zone of poorly compacted fill placed against a wall or cliff, using the eight-node CLE event tree. The probability of a continuous flaw is estimated by risk-factor and likelihood-factor scoring of three site factors — the compaction of the fill against the wall, the slope of the wall, and the presence of a concrete buttress — multiplied by a flaw-continuity probability keyed to the compaction of the fill, and an observation of leakage adjacent to the wall overrides it to certainty. The base of the flaw is placed at the crest elevation less the wall or cliff height. Initiation is evaluated by a Monte Carlo cylindrical-pipe hydraulic shear stress analysis, and continuation by the shared continuation method when a filter intersects the flaw. The methods shared with the other CLE screening models are documented in the CLE Common Methods Screening Technical Reference Manual.
