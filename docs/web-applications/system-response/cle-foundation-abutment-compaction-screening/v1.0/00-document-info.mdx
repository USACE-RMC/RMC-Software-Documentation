reportAbstract: Technical reference documenting the concentrated leak erosion (CLE) screening model for poor compaction along the foundation or abutment contact in the System Response library. The model computes a system response probability (SRP) at each headwater level from the eight-node CLE event tree. The probability of a continuous flaw is scored from three site factors (the preparation of the foundation or abutment surface, the method used to compact the embankment fill against it, and the width and width-to-height ratio of the embankment section) by risk-factor and likelihood-factor scoring, and is multiplied by a flaw-continuity probability keyed to the compaction method; a well-prepared surface forces the flaw probability to a negligible value. The flaw sits at the embankment-foundation contact, or for an abutment at an elevation one third of the way from normal pool to the crest. Initiation is evaluated by a Monte Carlo cylindrical-pipe shear stress analysis. This manual documents what is specific to the mechanism and refers to the shared CLE and continuation manuals for the common methods.
